# Economy model research

**2026-10-19.** Research by an Opus sub-agent for the phase 1 design gate (DECISIONS.md C9, P9). Not
yet a decision: Ben chooses the model. Sources at the end.

**Verdict.** No surveyed game economy is safe to copy. Doran and Parberry's bazaar drifts and leaks money; Veloren's planner creates goods and ran away; Victoria 3 creates and destroys goods by design. Recommended: **cost-anchored storehouse markets joined by news-driven flows**. Prices are a pure function of stock, so nothing integrates or drifts. Anchors come from recipes and terrain, so there are no price tables. Every transfer is two-sided integer arithmetic. Revised after SCALE.md: no cadence and no per-caravan agents off-screen; everything is piecewise-linear between breakpoints.

## 2. Recommended model

**Market state** (settlement, province or body; integers): professions and pop (observed); currency; stock `S[g]`; treasury `M[cur]`; rates and regime per good; error-diffusion accumulators; cached `L[g]`, `Sbar[g]`, `Mbar`; news ring; inbound and outbound flow lists. **Edge state:** per good rate `f`, quoted price `q`, travel time `d`, integer in-transit goods and money. **News:** immutable interned `(market, tick, S, rates, absorbable rate)`.

**Equations**
- Labor cost: `L_g = min_r (l_r + sum_i a_ri L_i) / y_r`, `y_r` capped by local resource flow `F_r`. Cycles solved once, `L = (I - A^T)^-1 l`, linted by Hawkins-Simon. Unproducible goods: `min_j L_g(j) + transport labor`.
- Anchor `p*_g = L_g / L_mint`: labor cost relative to mining one coin.
- Target `Sbar = T x max(c, y)`, `c = pop x need`.
- Price `p(S) = p* (1 + r (1 - S/Sbar))` on `[0, 2 Sbar]`.
- Production regimes, no chatter: below `Sbar` at capacity; at `Sbar` matching outflow; above `Sbar` zero.
- Money: mining while `M < Mbar = k pop / L_mint`; above `2 Mbar` cash refused (barter at anchor ratios).
- Flows: on an event, origin i allocates exportable rate to edges with news margin `p_j - p_i - tau_ij > 0`, best first, up to j's advertised absorbable rate (need minus inbound). `tau_ij = upkeep x d_ij / capacity`. Changes reach j after `d_ij`. Money flows back at `q`, re-quoted at either end's breakpoints.
- Player trade: `C = integral p dS`, closed-form quadratic in i128, buyer pays ceil, seller gets floor: path-independent, no pump.

**Breakpoints and catch-up.** Each good is linear in time within a regime; breakpoints are `S` crossing 0, `Sbar`, `2 Sbar`, `M` crossing 0, `Mbar`, `2 Mbar`, input exhaustion, deposit exhaustion, scheduled flow arrivals. Crossing time is `ceil((threshold - S) x den / rate)` ticks. `next_breakpoint()` is a per-market heap minimum, O(log G). Catch-up walks the breakpoints: O(breakpoints), independent of elapsed time. Reads never trigger decisions; only breakpoints and delivered events do.

**News** is not per caravan. Each breakpoint emits one delayed news event per incident edge (delay `d`); markets relay their freshest snapshots at their own breakpoints. Never a global price board (Gintis).

**Cost (ESTIMATE, to bench):** per event `O(log G + deg x G)`, ~1 to 5 us. CPU per sim day = event rate x that; a quiet world is free. Memory ~1 KB per market, ~100 B per active edge-good.

**Conservation:** per good, `sum S + in-transit + individual cargo = genesis + produced - consumed + player_in - player_out`; per currency likewise with mined and burned. Exact assert per event in debug.

**Clamps:** price +-r; stock `2 Sbar` plus in-transit; no debt; money bounds; caps on edges per market, news ring, snapshot arena; population fixed in phase 1. Stability rule (derived): `T >= 2 x max d` of active edges, so the band between `Sbar` and `2 Sbar` absorbs news lag.

**Local resources** enter only through `F_r`, hence `L_g`: crop growth from random ticks on observed farmland, tree, fish and ore densities, geodes, DeepTimeSites deposits, depletion.

**Player:** trades are events at the storehouse, limited by treasury, so dumping drains one town and flows carry the glut outward. The bridge reports catchment mining (raises `L_g`). Vanilla villager trades mint emeralds from nothing: route them through the treasury or ledger them as a player faucet (Ben's call).

**Currencies** are goods. Emerald ore spawns only in 10 mountain biomes (vanilla; pack worldgen unchecked), so most human towns import money. Amethyst clusters regrow about every 8,192 s per budding face, 4 shards each, so ribbits near geodes self-mint. Foreign currency anchors at mint parity `L_mint(foreign)/L_mint(home)` and floats within +- its transport cost (gold points). Border markets are the FX markets. Numismatics 1.1.0 ships no coin recipe (jar checked): spurs need an explicit sim mint against deposited emeralds at a fixed parity (Ben's call).

**Failure modes and tests**

| Failure | Test |
|---|---|
| Prices pinned at floor | Lint: each good has a producer and a sink; soak: share at a clamp below threshold |
| Leaks | Exact ledger assert |
| Price-cost loop (Veloren) | Anchors from recipes only; Hawkins-Simon lint |
| Cobweb from news lag | Two markets, one good: amplitude decays; event rate reaches zero in a closed world (quiescence) |
| Money pump | Property test: any closed trade cycle ends with no more money |
| Runaway growth | Soak asserts every cap |
| Read-time dependence | Property test: `catch_up(t0->t2) == catch_up(t1->t2) . catch_up(t0->t1)` for random `t1` |
| Nondeterminism (D&P chaos) | Same seed, 1 vs N threads, x86 vs ARM: identical hash |
| Deadlock | Lint: each need good has a route without traded inputs |

## 3. The six scale constraints

| Constraint | How it is met | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Lazy regions, `next_breakpoint()` | Regimes are piecewise-linear; crossings are closed-form; catch-up is O(breakpoints) | Dropped the daily staggered update |
| Composition | Integer ticks, integer state plus remainders, decisions only at intrinsic breakpoints. No randomness in the market; any shock (harvest, weather) commits at fixed slots keyed `(region, process, slot)` | Reads may not trigger re-solves |
| Flows | Edge rates change only at endpoint breakpoints or news; caravans/day = goods rate / capacity (2 trader llamas x 3 x strength slots). Visible caravans sampled from `(edge seed, departure slot)`; robbing removes that slot's cargo from in-transit | Dropped per-caravan arrival decisions off-screen |
| Prices only as news and flows | Allocation reads only news and edge quotes | None |
| Integers, error diffusion | Stocks, money, in-transit and rate accumulators are integers | None |
| Planet to galaxy | Same market on the ladder: fold sums `S`, `Sbar`, `c`, `y`, `M`, in-transit; `p*` is the output-weighted mean `L`; inter-province and inter-body edges sum rates. Cost scales with events, not markets | Interplanetary `tau` from launch cost, later |

## 4. Alternatives

**A. Fixed bazaar** (D&P with bazaarBot #17 fixes, bid >= ask check, estate-funded replacement) for observed settlements only. Visible NPC roles and job switching; but a sort per good per clearing, invented constants, chaos, and clearing needs a cadence, so it breaks laziness. Phase 2 LOD only.

**B. Global flow solve** (Victoria 3 ratio pricing plus a spatial-equilibrium LP when rates change, as SCALE.md does for districts). Smooth, no cobweb; but needs live cross-region prices, a global solve per change, and erases news.

## 5. Derived versus tunable

**Derived:** `L_g`, `p*`, mint parity, FX band, `tau`, `d` (path length / speed, movement attribute 0.175); pop and professions; caravan capacity; mining rates; Numismatics parity (smallest `p*` >= 1 spur); `T` lower bound.
**TUNABLE (labelled):** `r` (Vic3 uses 0.75); `T` above its bound; `k` cash days (Cambridge k); need basket unless tied to hunger rules; caps; path inefficiency; genesis endowment (ledgered).
