# Worm mods for the underground threat near oil (research, 2026-10-20)

Ben's ask (OG-9, DECISIONS.md): replace the sculk and warden gate with Terraria-style underground
worms that demolish players and their industry near oil fields, spare the oil pipes, and wreck
subterranean Create machinery, pumps and other works.

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.x, Create 6 pack. Sinytra Connector is in the pack.

How this was done: Modrinth API searches (about 60 queries, with and without the 1.21.1 and neoforge
facets), CurseForge via web search, then jars downloaded into `/tmp/worm-research/<mod>/`, unzipped and
read with `javap` and `strings`. Nothing from those jars was run. Jars of the pack's own mods were
scanned too (below).

## 1. Short answer

Nothing fits perfectly. No 1.21.1 NeoForge mod gives an ambient, stone-tunnelling, base-wrecking worm
that also spares well pipes by itself. The closest is **Terra Entity** (the Terraria monster module of
Confluence): its worms are real multipart worms that pass through terrain and break no blocks at all,
which is exactly the "bounded sim damage" shape rtsim already uses. Its cost is content and mixin
weight, not the worm. The lightweight alternative is **Aftershock** (Tremors graboids), which fits the
desert oil biome but is a single-body creature that burrows only in loose soil unless a tag is widened.

Ranked (details in section 3):

1. Terra Entity: best worm, heaviest mod.
2. Aftershock: light, tag-driven, wrong shape (not segmented), loose soil only.
3. Desert Behemoths: Sandworms (1.21.1 unofficial port): spectacular, but a desert-surface Dune worm.

Nothing in the pack already fits (section 5).

## 4. Recommendation

### 4.1 Pick

**Terra Entity, using only its `BaseWorm` family** (start with `devourer` and `giant_worm`, step up to
`bone_serpent` for the deep tier). It is the only 1.21.1 NeoForge worm found that is truly multipart,
goes through terrain, and breaks nothing on its own. That last property is the point: Ben wants the
well pipes spared and player works hit, and rtsim already decides damage itself (I23, P28: bounded sim
damage). A worm that never breaks blocks makes the plugin the single owner of what breaks, so sparing
pipes is a filter in the plugin, not a patch to someone else's mod.

Conditions on the pick, which Ben should weigh (D1 and the dislike of content):
- It drags in a large content mod. The mitigation is not to hide it but to keep it inert: stop its
  natural spawns, leave its NPCs and weapons uncraftable or unspawned. How much of that is possible
  without patching is UNVERIFIED (section 2.2). If it is not enough, the fallback is option 2 of 4.4.
- Its mixins (24 common) touch spawning, chunk tracking and block state. This needs a boot test with
  Sable, Create Aeronautics, Distant Horizons and the other mixin-heavy mods in the pack.

### 4.2 How the rtsim plugin would use it

A new plugin, `mic_rtsim_terraentity` (D4: plugins may be built around one mod; the core stays free of
it). The plugin owns four things:

1. **Trigger (player activity underground near an oil field).** The sim already produces the signals
   (OILGATE §4.5): the I2 census of districts, `BlockEvent.EntityPlaceEvent` naming the placing player,
   and `IND_DEPOSIT_SAMPLE` for chunks with a pumpjack hole. Add one more `Encroachment` kind,
   "works underground at a field": a player-placed block or machine, or a player present, below the
   local surface by a TUNABLE depth, inside an oil chunk or within a TUNABLE radius of one. Severity
   accumulates per district per day like the dragon rows, so one torch does not summon a worm but a
   mine, a pump room or a base does. Digging down the well shaft to oil from below counts, which is the
   cheese P13 item 3 exists to stop.
2. **Spawn (puppet).** Worms are sim agents with a class (new: Burrower) and the engine owns the truth
   (P3). When a player is near, the plugin materializes the agent as a puppet: create the worm type,
   place the head in solid rock a TUNABLE distance (for example 24 to 48 blocks) from the player's
   works and outside the exclusion volume of any well shaft, mark it persistent and tag it as a puppet
   so vanilla never saves it (parts are not saved anyway), and let it start in `idle`. Per-field
   concurrency is capped at 1 or 2. Strength tier (devourer, giant_worm, bone_serpent, wither) follows
   the district's accumulated severity.
3. **Steer.** Two layers, both vanilla `Mob` API, to be confirmed by GameTest:
   - Coarse: `setTarget(player)` and rely on its own `ComeAndBackDashAttackGoal` and
     `NearestAttackableTargetGoal`, bounded by the sim to a leash around the field (the plugin teleports
     or discards a worm that leaves it).
   - Fine: the plugin adds its own highest-priority `Goal` through the public `goalSelector` that
     drives the head toward a plugin-chosen waypoint (the nearest surveyed works, then the next), since
     the head's movement is ordinary `Mob` motion plus `noPhysics`. Waypoints come from the district's
     surveyed block set (INDUSTRY §13.7).
4. **Bounded damage, sparing the well pipes.** The worm breaks no blocks (verified), so every broken
   block is the plugin's decision. Each pass, the plugin takes the blocks within a TUNABLE radius of the
   head, intersects them with the district's surveyed player-placed set, removes the protected set, caps
   the count per pass and per visit (I23's cap and pacing, I25), and breaks the rest with
   `level.destroyBlock(pos, true)` so contents drop as items. The protected set:
   - the well shaft: every block in the column below each `createdieselgenerators:pumpjack_hole` down to
     bedrock, and the hole itself (OILGATE §3.1: the hole scans down "through pipes"; the exact pipe
     block ids are not yet listed in the docs, so confirm them in the CDG jar), and
   - a datapack block tag `#mic_rtsim:worm_immune` that lists those pipe blocks, bedrock, and
     `createdieselgenerators:oil_deposit`, so tuning is data, not code.
   Pumps, Create machines, drills, shafts, tanks and belts are in the surveyed set and are fair game, as
   Ben asked. A destroyed machine stops an abstract run (I23). Unwatched, the same selection applies
   abstractly and is applied on load under the block quota (C18); the watched and unwatched outcomes
   agree (P28). Fire and explosions are never used.
5. **Retreat.** A worm leaves when any of these holds: its per-visit block quota is spent, no surveyed
   works remain in reach, the player has left the field's radius for a dwell, a hard lifetime
   (TUNABLE) expires, or health is low. The plugin clears the target, steers the head down and away
   along a vector, and discards the puppet once it is a TUNABLE distance below the player's works or
   beyond tracking range. The sim agent returns to dormant with a cooldown, so there is no worm in
   permanent attendance. Retreat depth uses the same `noPhysics` motion, so it visibly dives.

All numbers above marked TUNABLE are placeholders to be set from GameTest and the sim's bench, not
findings.

### 4.3 Tests the plugin would need first (to turn UNVERIFIED into verified)

1. A GameTest that spawns `giant_worm` below ground, sets a player target 30 blocks away in stone, and
   records head depth and path (does it tunnel to the target or skim the surface?).
2. A boot test of Terra Entity alone, then in the full pack, with timings of server tick and load.
3. A test that the natural spawns can be silenced with the biome modifier override and config.
4. A test of contact damage against a villager, an rtsim puppet and a Create contraption rider.

### 4.4 If Terra Entity fails those tests

Closest options, in order of preference, not recommended as final:
1. **Aftershock graboid plus datapack.** Widen `#aftershock:graboid_burrowable` to stone and deepslate
   (it is a tag), extend `#aftershock:heat_blocks` with Create machines (it is a tag), and drop the
   spawn tags so the plugin alone places them. Gains: small footprint, desert-native (matches the oil
   biome rule), breaks heat blocks itself, config health and damage. Loses: not segmented, not
   Terraria-shaped, and its own block breaking is only partly tag-bounded (path breaking is not), so
   the plugin would still need to filter. Licence conflict (MIT vs CC-BY-NC-4.0) to settle.
2. **A reskinned existing mob.** Pack has Entity Model Features and Entity Texture Features, so a
   resource pack can reskin an existing multipart mob. The Ice and Fire death worm is already in the
   pack, multipart, and configurable in health and damage, but it can swim only through blocks in
   `BlockTags.SAND` (hard-coded vanilla tag), so it cannot enter stone without changing vanilla's
   sand tag, which has other users. Not recommended.
3. **A custom worm entity.** CustomHitboxLib (Modrinth `customhitboxlib`, MIT, 953 downloads, NeoForge
   1.21.1) offers multipart hitboxes on any entity, which would cut the work, but a bespoke entity
   conflicts with D3 ("only if absolutely necessary"). This is Ben's call, and only if sections 4.3
   tests fail and Aftershock is rejected.

### 4.5 Why not the others

- Desert Behemoths port: a Dune sandworm for open desert, made for spectacle (shaders, screen shake,
  thumpers); needs Lodestone and AAA Particles which the pack lacks; no evidence it enters stone.
  It could still serve as a surface desert raid body later, separate from this question.
- Devourer of Gods, Bosses'Rise, Alex's Mobs: bosses or large content mods, not an ambient threat.
- Enter Sandworm: wrong biome, Fabric-only jar.
- Sculk and wardens: Ben rejected (OG-9).
