# Choices: havens and ports that rise and fall (S8b)

Slice S8b of EQUILIBRIUM.md (§2.4.6a), started 2026-10-22 on branch `ports/lifecycle` after Ben's
answer to T37: "Huh, shouldn't ports be able to get raided, shut down, have new ones form, etc?"
Each row can be undone. Ben answered PORTS-1 and PORTS-2 on 2026-10-22 (EQUILIBRIUM.md §5.1,
DECISIONS.md): navies break havens (his exception, for havens only, to §2.4.5's rule that only
players break a faction), and trade ports close under blockade and reopen when it lifts (not the
recommendation). Ids: PC1 to PC10 the design, PC11 to PC19 the build of S8b-1, PC20 to PC25 the
blockade and closure, PC30 to PC39 the navy assaults (S8b-2), PC40 to PC49 the port raids, PC50
onward the closure's build.

No backward compatibility before a release: the save format stays `RTS2`, haven rows gain fields
without a migration (no saved world holds a haven: no plugin sends HAVEN_FOUND, T36).

| Id | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| PC1 | **An unwatched hunt pays its toll when the pirate reaches the lane**: the square law of its strength against the defence it judged the lane by (one merchant of the medium plus patrols and escorts, T35's D), `crew x (1 - square_law(A, D))` men in thousandths. The pool makes it good; what the pool lacks comes off the crew in whole men, rounded up. A pirate in view (embodied) pays nothing here: its real fights are reported. | The engine's fights are certain (Lanchester), so the cost of a hunt is the law's, not a roll; T35's D is the fight the pirate itself believed it was taking on, so belief and cost agree. One merchant per hunt is the least a hunt that takes any share must meet. |
| PC2 | **Fed for 30 days** (`ShipTunables::starve_days`): a haven is fed while one of its pirates began a hunt within 30 days; starving, its pool turns toward 0 at the hire rate (`pool e^(-r t / cap)`), one stored tick (`fed_until`) marking the turn. | Six rest-and-decide cycles of a pirate (a day's rest, a fruitless decision, a day's rest...) is long enough that a lane briefly escorted does not starve a haven, short enough that a haven with no prey does not sit manned for years. |
| PC3 | **A pirate is laid up at a fruitless decision while its haven starves**: crew ashore, slot empty with no refit booked. | Without it a starving haven's ships rest in port for ever; laying up at a decision needs no new event. |
| PC4 | **A shut haven** has no active ship and is broken or starving; it books no refit and the census counts it `broken`. A fed haven with every ship sunk is not shut (S8's refits still run). | Keeps S8's kill-all numbers: a player's kill-all is answered by refits, not by refounding. |
| PC5 | **Refound after `min(384, 48 x 2^P)` days** (`ShipTunables::refound`, half-life 96), the camps' rule (§2.4.5); one manned ship per medium and a quarter of the pool cap ashore; only out of view (no player's interest centre within `view_radius` of the dock) and only with a lane worth hunting for a fresh pirate, else again after 48 days. | Reuses the shared rule with the numbers EQUILIBRIUM already gives a broken faction site. The prey check stops a site with nothing to hunt from cycling through refound and starving. |
| PC6 | **New havens form only at found haven sites** (their own site, after it shut). | C22: the sim never makes a site. Bounds the haven count by the sites found. |
| PC7 | **A navy's assault force is its full complement** (`navy_ships x navy_crew x navy_strength`), the port's marines; no ship leaves its timetable and the navy keeps no losses. With the p > 2/3 gate the navy always wins outright, so losses would be bookkeeping only. 24 days is S1's census grid. |
| PC8 | **A haven's defence against an assault** is its men ashore plus the crews of its ships in port, at pirate strength and hull; ships at sea do not defend. | A navy that strikes while the fleet is out is the natural counter to a haven; it makes the toll matter (a worn pool is a weak haven). |
| PC9 | **A port raid is a coast raid's third target**, after a player district and a player's train: the port village at the hunted lane's nearer end, if unwatched, under the same roll (T25). | PC10 | **News is five new report kinds** in `rtsim-knowledge`, ids 16 to 20 of the range 16 to 23 the talk build reserved for ports and ships: PortRaided 16, Blockade 17 (begun), PortClosed 18, PortReopened 19, HavenFell 20. `who` the haven (the navy's port for 20), `whom` the port's settlement (the haven for 20), `place` the port (the haven for 20), severity 1 (2 for a closure and a fall). | PC11 | **A hunt that feeds a starving haven again wakes its slots** (`wake_slots`): every slot whose wait is over, laid-up ones included, books its refit at once; short of crew it waits for hires as S8's refits do. `check()` holds that a laid-up slot lies only in a haven that is starving or shut. | A laid-up slot books no refit (PC3), and a starving haven has no hire time to wait for, so without a wake a haven fed again by its one sailing ship would never man the rest. |
| PC12 | **The toll draws on the pool only beyond the crews its empty slots are owed** (T34, as top-ups do); the rest comes off the crew. | Measured with the toll on the whole pool: seeds 9 and 28 of `kill_all_pirates` never manned their empty sky slot within 120 days, because tolls held the pool at 2 to 4 men, below a sky crew of 5. | PC13 | **A fed haven whose last ship is sunk stays fed until `starve_days` after its first slot's refit is due** (`wait_on_refits`). | PC4 says a kill-all is answered by refits. | PC15 | **Laid up is a lost slot with no refit due** (`Status::Lost { back: LAID_UP }`, `u64::MAX`): crew 0, its crew into the pool (capped as captures are), its captain dead to the books, its hold kept and still held. **A ship in view (embodied) is never laid up and pays no toll.** A late or replayed FIGHT or DEMATERIALIZED report for a ship that is no longer active changes nothing (FIGHT carries no epoch, so the ship's state decides). | PC16 | **The haven row holds the lifecycle**: `fed_until`, `shut: Option<Shut { since, back, broken }>` (the design's dormant state), an `epoch`, and counters (`HavenCounts`: toll in thousandths, laid up, shuts, refounds), saved on the row (still `RTS2`).
