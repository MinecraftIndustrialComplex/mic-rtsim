# Choices: glass box phase G1 (2026-10-20)

**Branch `feat/glassbox-g1`.** Ben approved GLASSBOX.md and chose "Start building" (DECISIONS.md
GLASSBOX, G-B1 to G-B19, C32). G1 is the read-only compile and solve (G-B18, GLASSBOX.md §11.3): Java
extracts each Create district into a graph, the engine solves it for the floor L and the ceiling U per
good and checks it against one window of measurement; the replay still runs the C19 black box.
Reversible choices made to build it are numbered GB1 onward, one line of reason each. Every number
marked PLACEHOLDER is not measured; UNVERIFIED marks what no test has confirmed.

| # | Choice | Why |

The industry codec's writer and reader are shared (`pub(crate)`). | The i18 rework edits `IndRecords` and the industry contract test at the same time; separate files keep the merges clean, and one variant keeps the engine's match arms to one line each. |
| GB2 | **A node carries its rate table inline** (Rate24: level, recipe, batch, ticks as a rational), recipes are compile-wide records referred to by index, and a domain carries its levels (root speed as a rational, capacity in SU) and its members (ratio as a rational, impact). Stress quantities are f64, rates and speeds rationals. | One record per node keeps the count check simple. Recipes are shared because two machines of one kind share them. |
| GB3 | **IND_PROBE_SAMPLE (0x004D)** carries a probed buffer's contents, and it is **not counted** in the sweep's records. | §5.3 names IND_PROBE_SET but not how probe readings come back. Not counting them leaves the black box's sweep accounting (F5) untouched; a lost probe sample loses one localization point only. |
| GB4 | **The engine does not page the graph in G1**; Java resends a district's last compile every `glass.resendTicks` (PLACEHOLDER 1,200) with the RESEND flag, and the engine ignores a resend of a compile it already holds. | A dropped latest-wins record must be redone (§1.4), and a resend needs no new request kind. G1 is read only, so nothing is lost when a restart drops the graph until the next resend. G2, which replays from the graph, pages it with its district (A16). |
| GB5 | **IND_CHUNK_TICKING is journaled at 0x040B; IND_PROBE_SET is a command at 0x8406 without an op id.** | Ticking changes are events (I7 needs every one). Applying a probe set twice is harmless, so it needs no COMMAND_RESULT. |
| GB6 | **One frame per compile**, in IND_GRAPH_END: 0 for the level, else the Sable sub-level's id (the low 64 bits of its UUID's hash). Positions are the blocks' own coordinates. | Sable keeps a ship's blocks as ordinary block entities at fixed plot-grid positions in the same `ServerLevel` (`ServerLevelPlot`, 2,048 × 2,048 blocks per plot from x, z = 20,480,000); moving the ship changes only `SubLevel.logicalPose()`. So the plot coordinates are the ship's own frame and the extraction needs no transform (C32). A district never spans a ship and the world: they cannot touch. |
| GB7 | **IND_GRAPH_END carries the checksum window** (28 u32 `window`, 0 the engine's `glass.checksumWindowMin`), from Java's `glass.checksumWindowTicks`. | The engine's tunables are fixed per build; GameTests need short windows, and an operator can lengthen one district's without a rebuild. |
| GB8 | **A node flag PASS_THROUGH (64)** marks an in-place machine on a line: goods none of its recipes takes pass unchanged. | A press over a belt or a fan zone sees every item on the belt; without the flag each unmatched good would need a transfer recipe. |
| GB9 | **The graph's meaning is written down once**, docs/industry-boundary.md §9.3, before either half is built. | The extractor and the solver are built in parallel; one text keeps them agreeing on units, rate entries, heat, boilers, controllers and what is measured. |

## Solver and checksum (Rust), GB10 to GB29

| # | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| GB10 | **The LP is microlp 0.6.0** (Apache-2.0, pure Rust; its dependencies MIT or Apache-2.0, all compatible with AGPL-3.0-or-later), one LP per good and direction over the good's cone (the ancestors of the read stores' in-edges, or the descendants of their out-edges, stopping at stores that hold the good and have room), split into one small LP per connected part of the cone and summed, and not run at all when the linear bound already equals L (`Solution::lp_tight`). **The fallback is not a second simplex** but the routing-free linear relaxation (one backward and one forward pass with every split free), which is always a valid upper bound: U = min(LP, linear) when the LP runs, the linear bound alone when the LP fails or has more than `glass.lpVarsMax` (PLACEHOLDER 3,000) variables (`Solution::lp_skipped` counts them). | A licence-clean, dependency-light LP; a second simplex would only tighten a bound that is already safe, and the linear bound costs O(N + E). One LP over a whole district grew as N^1.4 (29 ms at 1,000 nodes); per part it is linear in the number of lines. |
| GB11 | **Rationals are i64/i64 with i128 intermediates, gcd-reduced**; a result that does not fit becomes the best lower rational approximation with a denominator of at most 2^31 (continued fractions, semiconvergents included), so L only rounds down; `_up` operations round up. The LP's f64 optimum becomes U as `x × (1 + glass.lpMarginRel) + 1e-9` (PLACEHOLDER 1e-6), rounded up on a 2^-24 grid, and U is never below L. | §3.7; a floor that rounds down stays a floor, and the margin covers the simplex's own float error. |
| GB12 | **Cycles: Tarjan components in topological order, each in the reverse postorder of a depth-first search from its entry**; goods edges that go back in that order carry nothing in L, and every good through a component of more than one node is bounded. The LP keeps the back edges (U is an upper bound only). | §3.4: a return loop carries nothing while downstream has room; L must stay a floor without a cyclic fixed point. |
| GB13 | **A store holds a good at `glass.holdingStacks` (PLACEHOLDER 1) stacks** (an item's max stack, 1,000 mB of a fluid, 1 unit else) and has room while its item slots are not all used (fluids: 1,000 mB free). A store the sampler does not read (no inventory index, or another revision's) acts as a buffer and its goods are bounded. A read store that holds its goods and has room decouples its inputs from its outputs for the classes. | A store with less than a stack can empty within a window; a buffer is what an unread store is to the flows. |
| GB14 | **Rules:** NONE and PREFER_FIRST with several out-edges are priority in `order`, PREFER_NEAREST too; SPLIT and ROUND_ROBIN share by water-filling; the FORCED modes give every side the slowest side's share; RANDOMIZE, SYNCHRONIZE, MERGE and SIDE_INSERT are bounded (L takes their fair share); ANDESITE_SPLIT gives one item per open side per entry of `n` (the first in-edge's `param`), the rest along the main line (the first out-edge). Filtered sides are served first. | GLASSBOX.md §3.3 rule by rule; the NONE case was unwritten (GB15). |
| GB15 | **§9.3 written where it was silent** (docs/industry-boundary.md): a controller's flag 2 is "on at compile"; a source with no recipes emits the goods its out-edges allow; `ticksNum` 0 is unlimited; an entry without a recipe is the node's shared capacity, and a machine's recipes are those its entries name; a heater's fuel is any good it is fed unless it has fuel recipes; a boiler selects the level with the largest selector at or below its level; a sequenced assembly is one SEQUENCED machine node; NONE with several out-edges is priority. | Each was a case the solver had to decide; the extractor must agree. |
| GB16 | **Heaters and boilers:** L's first backward pass assumes every ungated heater lit, then each round uses the duties of the round before; U takes a heater at duty 1 when it has remaining burn, is creative or sits in a cycle, else at its fuel supply. A boiler's U level is the highest running level any lit heater at full water selects. | The duty depends on the fuel the solve is still routing; rounds settle it. |
| GB17 | **Coupling rounds are capped at `glass.solveRoundsPerMultiInput` (PLACEHOLDER 2) × multi-input nodes + `glass.solveRoundsBase` (PLACEHOLDER 2)**, counting machines with a recipe of two inputs or more, nodes with two goods in-edges or more, and boilers that drive a domain. Acceptances only fall; a domain level change is a change. An unconverged solve makes every good bounded. | §3.2 step 4's `glass.solveRoundsMax`; each coupling can need two rounds to settle (one to back up, one to propagate). |
| GB18 | **Classes by taint over a union-find of everything a flow couples** (goods and heat edges, a driving boiler with its domain's members), split at read stores that decouple (GB13). Bounded flags, bounded rules, edge BOUNDED, cycles and unread stores make bounded; black boxes, ports, lines and drop edges unbounded. An exact good whose U is infinite is reported bounded. | §3.5: a good is judged against what can disturb it. |
| GB19 | **What the window measures and predicts:** per good, the summed stock of the read stores (each inventory once) minus the district's cumulative ports, as the learner's `N`; the prediction is the cumulative L, integrated per sweep interval in the regime read at its start, with a store's drain clamped to its stock. The verdict compares least-squares slopes of both series at the same sample times; σ is the larger of the residual's slope standard error and the model's `sqrt(6/5 × ∫λE[X²]dt) / W`; q is (largest batch in + largest batch out) / W; at least 3 samples. | Controllers change regime between sweeps, so only the per-interval integral is the time average; the residual of measured minus predicted does not count a regime change as noise. |
| GB20 | **The 6/5 factor** (`POISSON_SLOPE_FACTOR`): the least-squares slope of a cumulative compound Poisson count over W has variance 6/5 × λE[X²] / W. Derived (∫∫ w(s)w(t) min(s, t) over (W³/12)²) and checked by a Monte Carlo test (3,000 windows, within 10%). | The task's "about 1.2" is exact. |
| GB21 | **A window opens at the first live sweep at or after acceptance + `glass.warmupTicks` (PLACEHOLDER 1,200) + the compile's longest path latency**, and the next starts at the sweep that closes it. A sweep that is not live, a dropped sweep, an IND_EDIT, a session start or a district revision other than the compile's discards the open window and restarts the warm-up. Creative and overstressed live sweeps count. | §5.3; the compile models creative sources and overstress, and a revision change renumbers the inventories. |
| GB22 | **On a failed window:** the most optimistic failing good (smallest measured over predicted) names the limiting node: the nearest node with L flow upstream of the read stores' in-edges (produced) or downstream of their out-edges (consumed) whose own capacity binds, else the nearest machine. It is derated by η = measured / predicted (at most 1, floored on a 2^-20 grid after a 1e-9 relative nudge), and IND_PROBE_SET names up to `glass.probeNodesMax` (PLACEHOLDER 16) buffers and machines upstream of it, nearest first, sent only when the set changes. A pessimistic failure counts a strike and derates nothing. | §5.3, G-B14; the nudge keeps a float η of 0.4999999999 at 1/2. |
| GB23 | **Demotion:** after `glass.mismatchStrikes` (PLACEHOLDER 3) failed windows in a row the limiting node's model code is demoted to measured (a black box) for the district, kept across its compiles in engine state only (not persisted, GB4); strikes reset on a pass or a demotion. Store models are never demoted. | G-B14 "demote the node kind to measured"; the district is where the evidence is. |
| GB24 | **A regime solves once:** the solve runs at acceptance and again only when the regime key changes (FNV-1a 64 of held goods, room, read, gates, derates and demotions); the last `glass.regimeCache` (PLACEHOLDER 4) solutions are cached, so a cycling controller costs no solves after its first cycle. | D1: nothing per tick, solves only on compile or regime change. |
| GB25 | **Machines:** time-sharing water-fills in time across recipes (Σ c_r / cap_r ≤ 1); catalysts must be present (reachable) and are not consumed; with ANY_OUTPUT_BLOCKS every output must be accepted, without it a blocked output is lost; PASS_THROUGH goods share only the line's own capacity, not the machine's recipe rate. | §9.3's machine rules, one by one. |
| GB26 | **Kinetics in f64 as Create:** at a level, stress is Σ count × |ratio × speed| × impact, overstressed when capacity < stress (equal runs); STRESS_DISABLED never overstresses; a domain without a driver stays at its `current` level. | `KineticNetwork.calculateStress` and `isOverStressed`. |
| GB27 | **The report's window goods** are the last completed window's; before the first, the window in progress with verdicts NONE. Header slots 13 to 15 (reserved 0 until now) carry solves, demoted kinds and the longest path latency (`Report::to_f64s`). The layout is otherwise unchanged. | Operators see where a window stands; GameTests see whether a regime change re-solved. |
| GB28 | **Graph store bounds:** one assembly per district (a newer compile's record drops an older assembly, an older one is ignored), at most 2^20 records of a kind; a NOT_COMPILABLE END drops the held compile. IND_PROBE_SAMPLE keeps the first and latest total per probed node of the current set; IND_CHUNK_TICKING keeps a set per district, cleared at a session start. | Bounded state, no per-tick growth; a not-compilable district is the C19 box's alone. |
| GB29 | **`glass_nodes` and `glass_slack` in `rtsim-cli bench industry`** (`bench/glass.rs`): ten-node cells (store, funnel, two-input mixer, belt, two tunnels, three presses, store; one domain per cell) sent through `Engine::ingest`, timing assembly, build and first solve at 10 to 10,000 nodes; `glass_slack` forces both tunnels' splits so every good's U needs the LP. | GLASSBOX.md §9.3's contract on a factory with every kind of coupling the solve handles in G1. |
