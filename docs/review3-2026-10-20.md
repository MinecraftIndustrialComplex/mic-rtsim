The two pieces of the night's work that merged without an independent review:

- **Dragons:** `crates/rtsim-ecology` (merged at 468f85e, choices R1 to R31 in
  `docs/overnight-choices-dragons.md`, boundary in `docs/boundary.md` §6), and its hooks in
  `rtsim-travel`, `rtsim-world` and `rtsim-kernel`.
- **Phase 1 on the native engine:** `crates/rtsim-jni/src/engine/phase1.rs` and its folder,
  `rtsim-world`'s bridge API, the Java test surface and SPAWN_GROUP's `FLAG_SURFACE` (merged at
  8e6b286, choices V1 to V28 in `docs/overnight-choices-phase1jni.md`).

Every finding below is **CONFIRMED** (a test failed before the fix and passes after it; the test is
kept) or **PLAUSIBLE** (read in the code, no failing test). Severity is HIGH (goods or money
created or lost, or commands lost, on a path the design exists for), MEDIUM (wrong behaviour that
lasts, or an engine that cannot start), LOW (bounded or rare). Nothing here changes a design
decision; the last sections list what is Ben's to decide.

## Checked and found sound

- **Conservation with predation.** `take_stock` and `prey_on_traveller` book what they take as
  destroyed in the world's books; the dragons' ledger splits it exactly, hoards hold hoarded minus
  robbed minus lost, and `Dragons::check` holds along the property test and after every new test.
- **Op ids** for WRITE_GOSSIP, PLACE_HOUSE and SPAWN_RESIDENT come from identity (V14), so a
  re-derived command after a crash gets the same id. The three resend queues share one sequence
  counter, saved in the core's meta page.
- **Replay determinism:** every input is applied at the world tick it is logged with, and
  `log::apply` runs the world there first; the protocol test's state hash check still passes.
- **Boundary layouts:** the Java `NativeCodec` orders 16 to 24 match `orders.rs` field for field;
  SPAWN_GROUP's flags at offset 36 and `FLAG_SURFACE = 1` match on both sides and in boundary.md.
  The dragon codec's decode never indexes past its record (every read is checked) and rejects bad
  enums. Test-only orders are refused outside TEST mode.
- **Puppet duplication:** a dragon or caravan puppet is never materialized twice in a session (the
  engine checks embodiment or the puppet map before every spawn; a stale `Seen` is dropped by its
  leg generations); session starts make every dragon and phase 1 puppet abstract.

## Not fixed: for Ben

- **P3** above (what a dead or taken member takes with it): needs V13's reconciliation rule.
- **The phase 1 input log only grows** and every start replays all of it (V6's own "Incomplete"):
  start cost grows with world age, against SCALE.md §10. A snapshot codec for the world is the fix.
- **A phase 1 page that does not decode stops the whole engine from starting** (`Link::load`'s
  error ends `start`), where the industry link drops its pages and starts afresh (W23). Which policy
  phase 1 should follow is a choice; review 3's additions to the meta page are appended at its end,
  so pages written before them still load.
- **`World::visible` scans every touring band** on each call, and the native link now calls it
  every 20 game ticks for each player (SCALE.md §0). Bands need an index over time (their circuit's
  cells) to fix it.
- **Band puppets' members taken or killed are not booked** (V5 already notes the world counts 3
  members where the plugin spawns 4).
- **Dragons have no save codec** and are not wired into rtsim-jni yet, so their crash and replay
  behaviour cannot be reviewed. One case to design for then: an untagged spawn-egg dragon reported
  again after a crash (its DRAGON_ADOPT never reached a world save) would be adopted a second time.
- **A lairless dragon at rest is invisible to players:** it books no leg and is in no lair cell.
- **`PreyIndex::refresh`** rebuilds the whole prey index whenever a village or road is added (once
  per adoption, so quadratic over a world's life); `DistrictIndex::refresh` counts every district
  on each lookup; `World::node_of_key` (DRAGON_ATE) scans every node. All LOW; an incremental index
  needs add hooks in the world and the industry engine.
- **Breeding:** `pair` books a new breeding chain without a stamp, so a female paired again (only
  through `relocate` today) breeds on two chains.
