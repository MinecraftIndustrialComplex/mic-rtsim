One public SMP world is big enough: TBSMP Season 2, on archive.org

From two surveys on 21 October 2026: seven worlds this morning, an SMP search this afternoon. These worlds are for private local testing only; no world files are on this page.

TBSMP Season 2 (The Broken Script SMP) is public on archive.org with no login: an 8.67 GB zip that unpacks to a 13.5 GB world. It runs Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge with Create 6.0.6 and 144 mods, and 1,041 players joined it over its life. It is the only public world found that is both a multiplayer SMP and a Create 6 world, and it is far bigger than any other in chunks, track and Create machines. Only the rail counts lose: StressC has more trains and stations, StressA more stations and signals.

Only its overworld and nether load on our pack

  • 9 of its 11 dimensions come from mods we lack (The Broken Script, a CoD-style dimension mod, and three old copies). Keeping the overworld and nether leaves about 0.66M block entities, about 113.6k of them Create.
  • Where the trains are is unknown. The audit read the track file but not which dimension each train sits in, so the 55 may not all survive the cut.
  • Most of the overworld is not Create. Of its 633,008 block entities, 263,395 are FramedBlocks, 160,393 vanilla and 33,421 Lootr. Blocks from mods we lack become air; Create blocks survive.
  • Four patch releases behind: Create 6.0.6, against the pack's 6.0.10.

Decided: it becomes the next stress world

Its overworld and nether get imported onto the pack (Create 6.0.6 to 6.0.10) and run with and without rtsim, in pairs, like the earlier stress runs. It is queued behind today's builds. This can be undone, so it is not a question for you.

It answers part of what the generator was for (ST2): 112k Create block entities and 8,222 vaults in one overworld is real player-built industry, not a synthetic lattice.
