Your bar has two halves, and the gate fails the first

Your bar: a real oil pipeline must be infeasible to build, and the gate hard for a semi-casual player to cheese, for example by running in, dropping a minecart contraption as real blocks and running off laying pipe. The audit reads that as two halves:

  1. No standing oil works before guns. A well, a pipeline or a link home that keeps running is found, hurt and stopped, whoever or whatever built it.
  2. No cheap grab. The obvious tricks a semi-casual player tries cost real danger or real losses.

Danger to the player, from In Control or from field flights, can never meet the first half: neither breaks a block or drinks a tank. The first half rests entirely on the dragons' lairs, sensing, grudges and raids, and those have the three gaps above: wells never seen (A1 to A4), sensed late or not at all (B1, B2, B4), and sensed but not hurt (C1 to C6).

How the gate gets from a well to a raid

Every cheese breaks one of these links. The numbers are the gate's defaults with your reserve of 600.

  2. 2Known as a wellA loaded chunk of the district holds a Diesel Generators pumpjack hole.
  3. 3SensedOnly when a dragon arrives to drink: when its reserve is at 240 or below, at the oil chunk nearest to it. It senses districts whose centre is within 400 blocks, once a day each.Broken by group B
  4. 4GrudgedA drilling well, or 1,000 mB of fuel in its tanks, earns a grudge; a bare district does not. A drilling well's grudge lasts about 42 days, a fuel-only one about 15.
  5. 5RaidedA grudged district within 6,000 blocks: at most once every 3 days per dragon, up to 2 districts a raid. Unwatched, the sim resolves it; watched, the dragon comes for real.
  6. 6HurtTheft of the fuel stock, and up to 8 blocks open to the sky from the last survey. A survey runs only when every chunk of the district is loaded.Broken by group C
  7. 7Danger to the playerIn Control's desert dragons, lair dragons and watched raids. Field flights are off.Broken by group F

The two numbers that matter most: a resting dragon drinks, and so senses, every 45 game days (about 15 hours of play), and a drilling well stays a target for about 42 days once sensed. So a well is a target for about as long as a resting dragon takes to come back.

Pack facts every row leans on

  • Oil. The only crude source is the Diesel Generators pumpjack on an oil chunk. The pack puts oil only in high-oil desert chunks. Deep Time replaces that with petroleum traps anywhere.
  • The pumpjack pumps into its hole's 8,000 mB tank, and only while its chunk is loaded. In an rtsim world a district's flows are replayed while it is unloaded and settled into its tanks on load, so an unwatched well keeps paying.

Nine fixes, now being built

The smallest set that closes the open rows. None adds a new entity, block or item. Two change something you approved, and you answered both (F4, F8). The rest are code choices inside rtsim.

F1. A pumpjack starts its own district, whoever placed it

Closes A1 to A4

The pumpjack hole is the only way to pump crude, so seeing every hole sees every well. The Diesel Generators plugin records any hole it finds in a loaded chunk as a seed, on chunk load and from a hook on the hole's first server tick. A hole set down by a contraption, a ship, a Schematicannon or a Deployer is seen within a tick, and the rest of the dropped-off base joins by connection. It is a pumpjack-only exception to DS-10, the rule that only a player's own placement starts a district.

F2. The field watches its own ground

Closes B1 and B4

Instead of waiting for a dragon to drink nearby, a fire dragon from the nearest fire lair senses a district the moment it starts drilling, and once a game day for every district with a chunk within 400 blocks of a field. No flight is needed: it files the same report a visit would. Sensing then no longer depends on the reserve.

F3. Sense by chunks, not by the centre

Closes B2, and C3 for joined districts

Sensing checks every chunk of a district, not its centre, and the district index rebuilds whenever any district changes. A well joined to a base by pipe, belt, shaft or Ender Transmission is sensed as the well it is, and its raids pick from the whole district: linking a well to the base brings the dragons to the base.

F4. A roof delays a flyer; it does not stop one

Closes C1 and C2

Your answer (OGB-7).

The survey also lists the top block above each roofed machine when it lies within 8 blocks (a placeholder), marked open to the sky. A flyer breaks it like any open block, and the next survey finds the machine open. A shed buys a raid or two. More than 8 blocks of cover still shields, and the deep layer (F6) covers that.

F5. Survey what is loaded

Closes C3

A district bigger than the loaded area is never surveyed today, so raids on it break nothing. The loaded chunks are surveyed instead, and each chunk keeps its last survey until it is surveyed again. The perimeter cut, which needs the whole area, keeps the all-loaded rule.

F6. Build the underground half, as decided

Closes C4

OG-9e's Death Worm oil nest, asleep until a pumpjack drills. It is already your decision, and its hooks are in place.

F7. Name the builders

Closes C6

Sensing names the players who placed or broke blocks in the district in the last 10 days (a placeholder), as well as those whose home is within 512 blocks. A remote well's builder is then hunted at home and on sight, wherever they live. Each district keeps its last few editors, saved with it.

F8. Danger where the oil is

Closes cheese F1, and the first minutes of a grab

Your answer (OGB-6).

Field flights go on and In Control's fire dragon comes out, once F1 to F3 ship; the blazes stay. Before either is trusted at night, the danger test runs by night too (cheese F2).

F9. A watched raid does the bounded damage too, as P28 decided

Closes C5 and cheese F4

You decided P28 as "bounded damage too", but the build followed the earlier recommendation, so a watched raid only brought the real dragon. Now a watched raid also books blocks and theft after the fight, as an unwatched one does. Standing guard in a box keeps the player alive; it no longer keeps the well.

Not fixed, on purpose

  • Carrying oil home (group E). Once F1 to F3 make every well seen and hurt, what a player carries home is what they paid for at the well.
  • Killing the field's dragons with real weapons (B5) is the gate working: an emptied roost stands 20 days before a replacement comes from 3,000 blocks away.
  • Ethanol (D2): your answer, done in the pack, not in the gate.

Decided in the audit; each can be undone

  • F1 covers pumpjacks only. Its wider forms (any seed block that appears near oil without a player, or any machine anywhere, whoever placed it) would amend DS-10, and would be yours.
  • F2's sensing dragon comes from the nearest fire lair in reach of the field.
  • F4's roof reach of 8 blocks and F7's window of 10 days are placeholders.

Each fix brings its own tests: unit tests in the Rust engine for F2 to F5, F7 and F9, and for F1 a GameTest that sets a hole down with no player.

You answered all 3 on 21 October

Logged as OGB-6 to OGB-8. Two as recommended; for ethanol you chose your own way.

1. Is In Control's fire dragon still needed?

Your answer, as recommended: replace it.

When rtsim ships with F1 to F3, field flights go on at the built draw, In Control's fire dragon rule comes out, the blazes stay, and the danger test's "at least as dangerous as In Control" pass condition is retired. Danger then follows the deposits, from one system whose dragons drink, sense and remember (OGB-6).

2. Does a roof stop a flying raid?

Your answer, as recommended: the roof is breakable.

A roof within 8 blocks above a machine is itself a block a flyer can break, so a shed buys a raid or two. This replaces OG-11's full shield (OGB-7).

3. Ethanol: behind oil, or the pre-oil fuel?

Your answer: "Pre-oil but re-balance it to make it kinda shit as fuel for diesel engines. (I'm pretty sure if we make it realistic it'll balance itself.)"

Ethanol stays the pre-oil fuel, with its fermenting and moonshine routes, made realistic: low energy per litre and very low cetane, so it is poor in diesel engines. The rebalance is being done separately in the pack, not in the gate's build (OGB-8).
