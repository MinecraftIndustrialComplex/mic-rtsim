From a dead player to a ridden sled in seven steps

  1. Craft a Butcher's Knife

    Iron ingot
    Iron ingot
    Stick
    →
    Butcher's Knife

    A shaped recipe: two iron ingots on top, a stick under the left one. Any item in the tag meat_toboggan:carving_knives carves too, so other mods' knives can be added.

  2. A player dies, and their ragdoll stays as a corpse

    The body from Sable: Player Ragdoll lies where they fell, wearing their gear, for 5 minutes (corpses.seconds). Without this addon the ragdoll mod removes it on death.

  3. Right-click the corpse with the knife: each swing cuts off one limb

    Knife in the main hand. It takes the limb you clicked if it is still on, otherwise the next in this order:

    Right arm→ Left arm→ Right leg→ Left leg

    Each cut sprays blood, squishes and makes the body twitch, and drops a Severed Arm or Severed Leg whose tooltip says "Belonged to <name>". 8 ticks between cuts; the knife loses durability.

    Severed Arm
    Severed Leg

  4. The fifth cut opens the belly and makes the toboggan

    It pulls the intestines out, and the torso and head become a Meat Toboggan in the victim's own skin, named "Meat Toboggan of <name>".

    Meat Toboggan of <name>

  5. Right-click to sit on it and ride

    W pushes, S brakes, A and D steer, Space hops. Two intestine reins run from the open belly to your hands, and it leaves a fading bile and gore trail. The victim's head turns to stare at you, or at the nearest player when nobody rides.

    3.2

    blocks a second top speed (1.4 a tick), from hills and drops

    Worked out from the code's constants, not timed in game. Each landing off a drop on snow turns the fall into forward speed.

    Hitting a wall fast hurts the rider; running something over hurts it. Baby villagers flee as it passes.

  6. Lead it, or tie it behind a horse

    Use a lead on it to lead it like an animal. Then sneak and use a horse to tie every toboggan you are leading behind it. Donkeys, mules, skeleton and zombie horses, camels, llamas and trader llamas work too (tag meat_toboggan:can_tow). Ride the horse and it drags the toboggan on a rope, or sit on the toboggan and drive the riderless horse by the reins:

  7. Hit it to pick it up

    It drops as an item that keeps the victim, and places again like a boat.

    <victim> was carved into a meat toboggan by <you>
    <rider> went face first off a meat toboggan
    <mob> was run over by <you> on a meat toboggan

    Three of the mod's own death messages.

From SledMath: speed kept per tick (0.993, 0.988, 0.982, 0.9, 0.7), over 20 ticks.

Two empty block tags, meat_toboggan:glides_like_snow and glides_like_ice, let other mods' blocks join the fast rows.

The art is real Blockbench, rebuilt by one command

Every model and texture is made in the real Blockbench, run headless by the scripts in tools/blockbench/. tools/blockbench/run.sh rebuilds all of it: the toboggan in wide and slim skins, the Java model generated from Blockbench's export, the knife, the limb sprites, the item and the logo.

The pictures use Minecraft's default Steve and Alex skins, and Blockbench's own reference player for the knife in hand. No server players.

The realism pass rebuilt the anatomy

After your "not nearly realistic enough": bone shards in the stumps, bulging stumps, a ribcage in the open belly, and flesh painted texel by texel on a layer four times as dense as the skin.

A limbless corpse on its back, sliding head first

The head tips back to stare at the rider. The belly is open over the ribcage, a coil of guts sits where the reins leave, and the stumps show bone. The skin layer is the victim's real player skin, wide or slim.

A cleaver-style butcher's knife

A bevelled blade with scratches and blood at the heel, on a riveted wooden handle, with a 64 by 64 texture.

Severed limbs show the cut

Hand-drawn sprites. The cut face shows skin, a ring of fat, muscle and bone. The arm wears a torn sleeve and ends in a hand; the leg wears jeans and a shoe.

Nine choices I made while you slept, each reversible

  • Corpses on death are on by default, for players only. Mob ragdolls cannot be carved.
  • Carving living players is off by default.
  • The knife recipe: two iron ingots over a stick.
  • Vanilla sounds only: powder snow, slime, axe strip, honey and a big fall.
  • What can tow: horse, donkey, mule, skeleton horse, zombie horse, camel, llama, trader llama.
  • Local only: no GitHub repo, not added to the pack.
  • Spelling: meat_toboggan and "Meat Toboggan", not "tobaggan".
  • One rider per toboggan.
  • The item shows a small copy of the sled in the victim's skin.

Seven known limits

  • The head and stumps can poke into walls. The collision box is a 1.375-block square; the model is about 2.7 long and 1.75 wide. A boat's ends do the same.
  • The 16 by 16 inventory sprite is hand-drawn, so it will not follow future model changes.
  • In an item frame the head pokes slightly past the frame.
  • The shoe on the severed leg reads as a dark blob at normal size.
  • Driving a camel by the reins is fixed in code but not tried in game.
  • Sable sometimes never builds collision for freshly loaded GameTest chunks, so a ragdoll sinks through the floor in a test. It is a Sable or ragdoll mod quirk; the tests work around it.
  • If a revive mod cancels a death after a ragdolled player's body has been detached, the corpse stays anyway. That detach has to run before the ragdoll mod's own handler.

Two decisions wait on you

1. Put Meat Toboggan in the MIC pack, with a GitHub repo and releases?

Recommended: not yet. Keep it local, as you said, until you have played it.

Today it is a local repo with no remote. Going into the pack would mean a repo and releases like the pack's other custom mods. Nothing has been done toward that.

2. Turn on carving living players for the server?

Recommended: leave it off, the default.

With carving.livingPlayers on, anyone with a knife can dismember a player who is ragdolled but alive; each limb hurts and the last cut kills. The review's three fixes for it are in, so it works either way.
