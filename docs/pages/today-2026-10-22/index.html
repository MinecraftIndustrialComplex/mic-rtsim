Oil F6: the Death Worm nest Landed

74c99c1, choices OGC70 to OGC103 in docs/choices-oilgate.md, built to your OG-9 answer (e).

Drilling oil now wakes a Death Worm nest under the field, and the nest keeps sending worms while the drilling goes on. Nests are on by default (placeNests, flag 4 of the oil config).

  • WhereOne nest for each 8 by 8 chunk cell with oil: a 9 by 9 chamber 20 to 60 blocks under the first oil chunk, carved only into natural ground (8 tries to find some).
  • The heartA vanilla spawner set to the Ice and Fire Death Worm that never spawns by itself (player range 0). The engine alone renews the worms.
  • WakingA nest sleeps until a pumpjack's deposit sample, from the district's latest sweep, lies within 128 blocks of it.
  • WormsThe first awake day hatches 2. After a worm is lost, one comes back after 12 drilling days, doubled for each step of pressure players have put on the nest, to at most 96. Kills add pressure, and it halves every 96 nest days. The nest's stage follows the drainage in thirds, and sets the worms' size; a bottomless well keeps it at stage 1.
  • Breaking the heartRenewal stops and the pressure rises by one; the worms already there stay and still raid. The heart reforms after 96 drilling days, doubled for each step of pressure, to at most 384. A nest nobody drills never reforms.
  • RaidsA chance of 250 in 1,000 each drilling day, as a new fifth threat class, Burrowing, that walls, turrets and guards do not stop. Each worm breaks one machine or store block, underground ones first, and carries nothing off.
  • ArmourA worm never breaks a block that stands on obsidian, crying obsidian or reinforced deepslate within 4 blocks below (the #mic_rtsim:armoured_floor tag), and never the pumpjack's own hole.
  • With a player thereThe worms glide through the ground, stop under armour or surface, fight the nearest player within 48 blocks for 1,200 ticks, then glide home. These worms break no blocks.

Saves move to RTD6 and RTN6, with no migration. The nest GameTests pass on the stub and on the native engine. OGC103 also fixed the dragon steering test, which now sets morning; the Ice and Fire GameTests pass 28 of 28.

Adopting a district no longer grows with the world (DS-12) Landed

313f1c0, from 598d978 and 6170350.

/rtsim district adopt used to run a detection over every district, so one adopt held the tick longer the more districts a world had. It now detects only around its own chunks (Districts.adoptAround), with the same budget as a ticked pass, so it costs one neighbourhood's detection on any world. The adopt GameTests check three situations against a full detection. Merge, split and exclude still run a full detection.

mic_climate: the first-tick stall is fixed, not yet released Landed

mic-climate master 6d875a4, the fix in 16988a6.

The first temperature query in a region nobody had loaded stalled the server tick for 1.2 to 3.6 seconds. Project Atmosphere 0.9.1.2 built a new RandomState for every forecast square, and C2ME compiled its density functions again each time. mic_climate now shares one RandomState for each server (BiomeSamplerMixin). Samples taken on the server thread went from 190 to 0. The GameTests pass 37 of 37 with Project Atmosphere and without it, and 1,875 sampled points are identical before and after. What remains is the one build at start-up.

The pack still pins mic_climate 0.2.0, so players do not have the fix yet.

Release mic_climate 0.2.1? For you

  • (a) Yes (recommended): raise the version, build, run both GameTest runs again, publish the GitHub release and repin the pack.
  • (b) Not yet: keep 0.2.0 in the pack and the fix on master.

Yours because it is a release and changes the pack.

EQUILIBRIUM S8: pirates keep sailing Landed

e442612, choices T36 to T39.

sim ships --days 1000 now keeps both pirates on all 30 seeds, still sailing on day 996.7 or later, with 271 to 356 hunts a seed. Before, no seed had a pirate left after day 77.6. A fight report that arrives late can no longer strand a pirate (T39). The equilibrium page has the plan this is part of.

Should unattended havens lose crew? For you

A haven nobody visits keeps every pirate slot manned (1.00), above the plan's band of 0.83 to 0.94 (T37). Bringing it down would need a new kind of loss: a rolled chance, or fights with nobody there, both of which the engine's rules forbid.

  • (a) Keep it as is (recommended): a haven nobody watches stays fully manned. The test holds the band's floor, not its ceiling.
  • (b) Enforce the band's top: add a loss mechanic, with a rule change to allow it.

Yours because it would change one of the engine's rules.

G2-9: each district's glass state is its own page, and saves write only what changed Landed

G2-9 in 0bc2f02 (choices G2-80 to G2-85, docs at 903190a); the saves in b5cd4d2 (choices ID1 to ID9 in docs/choices-industry-dirty-pages.md).

The glass works' state is now saved a district at a time, as a page of its own (table 0x4903, magic IGL1). A district loaded back replays exactly as before, and the compile numbers survive a restart (G2-81). No migration; the GameTest worlds were deleted.

That made saves bigger (G2-83): every industry page was written whole at every save, and a glass page is about twelve times a district page. So the coordinator took G2-83's option (a) while you were away, and it landed this evening: a save now writes only the pages that changed since the last one, plus the small globals page. A quiet district costs nothing to save.

Quiet districts replaying 10-node factories, beside 10 busy export lines whose 30 pages change every day; those and the globals page are the 17.5 KB. The save's time on the step thread at 10,000 went from 396 to 527 ms to 97 to 161 microseconds, flat in the district count. The heap and the CPU for each sim day did not change measurably (a loaded laptop). Blank: not measured.

  • A restart writes every page once: the first save after it is as big as before; from the second on, only changed pages.
  • A missed change cannot slip by quietly: a check mode hashes every page at each save and fails if one changed without being marked. It ran through the industry tests, the G2-9 restore tests and the benches, and found none.
  • The page format did not change, so no save was wiped.

Confirm: save only the pages that changed For you

Built and landed, but provisional until you confirm it. Going back is one call in the save path; the format is the same either way.

  • (a) Keep changed-page saves (recommended).
  • (b) Keep them and also slim the glass page, perhaps a quarter smaller. It now matters only for the first save after a compile or a restart.
  • (c) Go back to writing every page.

Yours because it changes how a world is saved.

One more thing to know, not to decide: the globals page is still written whole at every save. It is 105 to 128 bytes today, but it grows with the districts that are surveyed or due, so a large server with many of them would write it every time. Splitting it into pages is the next step if it ever shows in a save's size.
