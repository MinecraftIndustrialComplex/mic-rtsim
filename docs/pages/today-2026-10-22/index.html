Oil F6: the Death Worm nest Landed

74c99c1, choices OGC70 to OGC103 in docs/choices-oilgate.md, built to your OG-9 answer (e).

Drilling oil now wakes a Death Worm nest under the field, and the nest keeps sending worms while the drilling goes on. Nests are on by default (placeNests, flag 4 of the oil config).

  • WhereOne nest for each 8 by 8 chunk cell with oil: a 9 by 9 chamber 20 to 60 blocks under the first oil chunk, carved only into natural ground (8 tries to find some).
  • The heartA vanilla spawner set to the Ice and Fire Death Worm that never spawns by itself (player range 0). The engine alone renews the worms.
  • WakingA nest sleeps until a pumpjack's deposit sample, from the district's latest sweep, lies within 128 blocks of it.
  • WormsThe first awake day hatches 2. After a worm is lost, one comes back after 12 drilling days, doubled for each step of pressure players have put on the nest, to at most 96. Kills add pressure, and it halves every 96 nest days. The nest's stage follows the drainage in thirds, and sets the worms' size; a bottomless well keeps it at stage 1.
  • Breaking the heartRenewal stops and the pressure rises by one; the worms already there stay and still raid. The heart reforms after 96 drilling days, doubled for each step of pressure, to at most 384. A nest nobody drills never reforms.
  • RaidsA chance of 250 in 1,000 each drilling day, as a new fifth threat class, Burrowing, that walls, turrets and guards do not stop. Each worm breaks one machine or store block, underground ones first, and carries nothing off.
  • ArmourA worm never breaks a block that stands on obsidian, crying obsidian or reinforced deepslate within 4 blocks below (the #mic_rtsim:armoured_floor tag), and never the pumpjack's own hole.
  • With a player thereThe worms glide through the ground, stop under armour or surface, fight the nearest player within 48 blocks for 1,200 ticks, then glide home. These worms break no blocks.

Saves move to RTD6 and RTN6, with no migration. The nest GameTests pass on the stub and on the native engine. OGC103 also fixed the dragon steering test, which now sets morning; the Ice and Fire GameTests pass 28 of 28.

Adopting a district no longer grows with the world (DS-12) Landed

313f1c0, from 598d978 and 6170350.

/rtsim district adopt used to run a detection over every district, so one adopt held the tick longer the more districts a world had. It now detects only around its own chunks (Districts.adoptAround), with the same budget as a ticked pass, so it costs one neighbourhood's detection on any world. The adopt GameTests check three situations against a full detection. Merge, split and exclude still run a full detection.

Ports: answered, being built Being built

Asked whether havens nobody visits should lose crew, you asked back: "Huh, shouldn't ports be able to get raided, shut down, have new ones form, etc?" The port lifecycle that came of it asked you two things, and you answered both:

  • PORTS-1, "Navies too". A navy can break a pirate haven while nobody watches, but only when it believes it will win, and the result is certain, not rolled. A broken haven is founded again at its site after up to 384 days. This is an exception to "only players break a faction", for havens only.
  • PORTS-2, "Close under blockade". A trade port whose every lane has been at a haven's full danger for 30 days lays up its ships until the blockade lifts. Pirates also raid port villages for their market stock.

The first part, havens that starve, shut and are founded again, landed this evening (966d7a93); navies, port raids and closing under blockade are being built.

EQUILIBRIUM S4: dragons come back to empty lairs, slower where you hunt them Landed

SEC-1: factories split into sections at their links and stores Being built

SECTIONS.md on mic-rtsim main; your answers recorded there and in DECISIONS.md (9e1457ff).

The design for your "Cut at links and stores" came back today, and you answered its six questions, each with the recommended option. Districts stop joining merely because their chunks touch: only a physical link joins them. Inside a district the factory is cut at its stores into sections, each a working line between stores. A section learns, replays and goes stale on its own, and the district stays the unit of loading. An edit stales only the sections around it, and a replay that fails freezes only the sections it names while the rest go on.

Your six answers

  1. Shaft networks are a shared input that sections cut through, so one network running a whole building does not make the building one section.
  2. Rotation across districts: districts on one shaft network stay apart but replay together. Past 16 districts on one network they are not coupled, and each learns its rates on its own.
  3. A store shared by sections: what goes in and out is counted at Create's movers (funnels, chutes, arms, basins, frogports, packagers and pipes). Hoppers and other mods' movers there are not counted, and their sections are judged together.
  4. Packages between districts sit in a package store owned by no district, with its own saved data.
  5. A chain with one end loaded: packages are modelled both ways, the G2-11 way. They wait on the last loaded conveyor and the far district's replay draws them; a reset leaves at worst a duplicate, never a loss.
  6. Packagers are not cuts: the cut is at the chain, and a packager's packages are counted at the frogport or postbox they pass through.

The build has started, slice by slice. The first of seven slices, under way now, stops the compile, Create's chain conveyors and Create's network recount from loading chunks, which today can pull a district nobody is near into a partial load. Saves get new formats with a loud reset and no migration, as for everything before release.

The 200-district stress run again: rtsim is about 1.3% of a tick Landed

33ce3e55; docs/stress-worlds-results.md section 10.9, choices SG43 to SG49. Run on main at b5cd4d2.

The synthetic world our own generator builds: 200 districts, 6,400 factories and 360 cargo trains, run with and without rtsim side by side, and once with rtsim on both sides as a control.

  • Cost: rtsim takes 1.6 to 1.7 ms of a 122 to 133 ms tick, about 1.3%, inside the control's noise (its two identical servers ran 3.3 ms apart).
  • The old measure missed most of it. neoforge tps is measured before the end-of-tick handlers run, where most of rtsim's work is. A new whole-tick measure catches them, and its difference matches rtsim's own cost to 0.03 ms.
  • Detection is spread out: the periodic pass now runs over 363 ticks, and the worst rtsim tick fell from 201 to 230 ms to 34 to 51 ms.
  • Stations: all 720 learned both their load and their unload, none wrong. Factory chests changed only by their own machines' work.
  • The first-tick stall was still there, as expected: the run used mic_climate 0.2.0, and the fix is 0.2.1.

Found, and being fixed now

  • Trains made edges from a station to itself when a station's district was not yet declared. Fix: the bridge reports a stop at a station nobody watches, so no false edge starts; and adopts that wait now join the detection already running.
  • The one-call rescan got two to three times slower. Fix: budget it over ticks, after a profile finds what grew.
  • The globals page is rewritten whole on every save and grows with the line legs it holds. Fix: the legs get pages of their own, so a save writes only the changed ones.

The coordinator picked these fixes; each can be undone.
