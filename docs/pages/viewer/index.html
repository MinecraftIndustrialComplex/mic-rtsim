Rail is real rail: Create's own track, curves and all

You said: "Connections between train lines are just straight lines."

  • The track comes from Create's own create_tracks.dat, curves included.
  • rtsim's train lines follow that track instead of cutting straight across.
  • Tap a train line and its card reads like "A to B: coal, 3 trips a day".

A plain map, not a dashboard

You said the map and its UI and UX looked like "AI slop".

  • No glassy panels, gradients, emoji or spaced capitals.
  • One status line, like "Save, day 3,467 at 11:46. Nothing moves."
  • The world's own name, read from level.dat, in the corner.
  • Layers the world does not have fold away under "Not in this world".
  • A layer that only draws closer says "zoom in" instead of showing nothing.
  • Labels never cover a symbol, another label, the key or a card. A name already shown nearby is not repeated.
  • Create's default names, "Track Station" and "Unnamed Train", are not printed.
  • Light and dark themes. Laptop first, phone second.

Furnaces show only inside districts

Your second screenshot, of Taiga 1 to 4, had 168 furnace marks labelled "Furnace" and "2 furnaces", where the game holds no district.

  • A furnace of any mod, or any vanilla block, is drawn only inside a district. On its own it belongs to a house, not to industry.
  • Inside a district they group by what they do: "Smelting: 9 furnaces, 1 smoker". A lone one gets no label.
  • Machine names appear only at the closest zooms.
  • The game side is DS-10: a furnace will never start a district. It is on its branch and not merged yet. Until it lands, the few districts the old rule started from a furnace still show (item 6 below).

Trains look like trains

  • Each car is as long as it really is. Cars on a single bogey are spaced by the train's carriage spacing.
  • A train's card gives its speed in blocks a second, where it is bound, the distance left, its cargo and its schedule.
  • Schedule waits merge into one phrase: "wait 10 s and for redstone".

Factories you can read

The old map showed no industry at all. The new one reads machines, power, arms, gauges, storage, belts, chain conveyors and package ports from the world's block entities. It loads them only when you zoom in, so a big world never gets a full scan up front.

On the mapMeans
CircleA processing machine
TriangleA power source

Red ringIts kinetic network is overstressed (cards give stress in su)
Gauge, green or amberA factory gauge whose stock is met, or short

District cards say what a district holds first, then list its stations by name. Their state is in plain words: "Unloaded, run by rtsim" or "Stopped: a machine was destroyed".
