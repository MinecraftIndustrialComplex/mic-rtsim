15 ways to make goods twice, all on main today; 10 fixes are built

Each could make goods twice after a crash or a module switch. They come from two causes.

Cause 1

A save wrote its files in an order nobody controlled

Before create_tracks.datCDG oil filertsim journalregion files in any order
After U-a the othersthenrtsim journal, last

A crash between those writes could make goods twice. U-a writes the journal last, and only during a full world save; it is built and lands third.

Cause 2

A switched-off module ignored answers

Before: while a module was off, the engine ignored answers, then resent them later, after Java had forgotten them.

After option A: the engine hears answers whatever the switch, and their effects apply at their own tick at switch-on. One rule for the whole engine: a switch only stops new decisions.

Data formats, 5

Pre-release, no migrations, and all safe to uninstall: the keys drop.

  • Trains: a per-train record of applied ops in Create's train NBT, via a mixin. Up to 4,096 entries per train, about 2 KiB typical.
  • Diesel oil: a record in CDG's oil SavedData, via a mixin.
  • Destroy pollution: a mic_rtsim level attachment. NeoForge skips unknown attachments after uninstall.
  • Caravans: the module's saved state gains a capped list of records kept while it is off.
  • Fixture bumps: RTC7, RTC8, RTD8, RTN8, META 6, META 7.

Design, 9

  • Journal written last, and only during a full world save. A single-dimension save from another mod leaves it for the next full save.
  • One rule for the whole engine: a module switch only stops new decisions. Every record of what already happened is still heard, at its own tick.
  • The save guard keeps waiting while a stuck chunk can still be saved, instead of giving up after 3 rounds. At server stop it skips the journal write rather than risk a duplicate. New counters in /rtsim perf.
  • Records are always saved and loaded, whatever the module switch says.
  • Industry pauses while replaying. This reverses an earlier replay-gate choice.
  • Exchange and package op ids leave out the amounts. A re-decision with a different amount is treated as a repeat, so only the difference can be lost, never doubled.
  • No despawn of dragons at a dragons switch-off: players would see them vanish. Recording the death is enough.
  • No Java-side freeze of caravans at a switch-off, for the same reason.
  • Option A lands alone first, to break a landing deadlock between the train fix and the journal change.
