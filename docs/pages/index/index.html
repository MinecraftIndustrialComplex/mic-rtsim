Waiting on you: 7 groups need an answer, 28 choices stand unless you object

  • 4 decisions on rtsim for any pack

    Whether "any pack" is a goal, how storage networks and wireless links are modelled, the machine model for non-Create mods, and the release shape. G0 to G3 get built either way: G1 and G2 fix MIC, G0 serves the addon. Recommendations on the page.

  • 4 decisions on the standalone Create addon

    What goes public, the license, the name, and when. Recommendations on the page.

  • 7 decisions on the Mowzie's Mobs plan

    Where the conflict module's Java half lives (MZ-1, which blocks every conflict plugin), what comes back after a boss dies and which Mowzie's items stay (MZ-4), what an ambush does to a train (MZ-5), whom a line's grudge falls on (MZ-13), the grove's people (MZ-11), which bosses leave home (MZ-3) and the tribute (MZ-6). Nothing is built. Recommendations on the page.

  • 6 decisions on population equilibrium and renewal

    How an emptied village comes back (EQ-1, which reopens your "no villagers for now"), whether a roosting dragon is a saved vanilla mob (EQ-2, which reopens P3), where the site ledger lives in the world save (EQ-3), how fast a killed Frostmaw returns (EQ-4, the delay MZ-4 defers to), when factions fight (EQ-5) and whether one renewal rule binds every kind (EQ-6). Nothing is built. Recommendations on the page.

  • 5 questions on the oil gate's dragon half

    What makes the gate's own dragon flights dangerous enough to switch on (Q1: a bigger draw and a re-measure over 5 runs a site), the fuel reserve's size (Q2: 600 rather than today's 100, which lasts about three streams), whether drinking stays free of the theft cap (Q3), whether a lost visitor's visit ends (Q4) and a cheap biome check before the oil lookup on new chunks (Q5). It is built and runs on today's settings until you answer. Recommendations on the page.

  • 4 stress-world follow-ups, recommended but not built
    • Server heap for big Create worlds: -Xms equal to -Xmx, at least 8 GB.
    • Tell players not to bring 1.19.2 worlds, which lose item data on upgrade.
    • Put cargo trains in the synthetic stress generator (station learning has only seen a passenger metro).
    • Spread the 5-minute district rescan over several ticks.
  • 9 deploy items (DR1 to DR9), on hold under DR-0

    They come back when you restart the server deploy. DR1, an Ice and Fire crash, blocks it; the others are heap, watchdog, deploy order, pack scripts and the glass box setting on the host.

  • Low priority: TP19

    Should Deep Time get a public layout API? It overlaps the addon's deeptime-format question.
