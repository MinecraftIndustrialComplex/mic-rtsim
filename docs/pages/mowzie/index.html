A Frostmaw that hunts your trains and a tribe that wants your gold: the plan is ready, and 7 decisions are yours

The Mowzie's Mobs faction plan, MOWZIE.md on mic-rtsim main (e4b7a3a). It went through three adversarial reviews against the Mowzie's Mobs 1.8.2, Create 6.0.10 and engine sources, then three further checks. The third review found every earlier problem fixed and one blocker, since fixed. The fourth and fifth checks found small gaps in how a camp counts its bodies, now fixed. The sixth found that nothing told the game a war party had left home or come back; two new records fix that, and the fix has landed. A seventh check is running. Nothing is built.

Two Mowzie's bosses become rtsim factions. A Frostmaw in the snow comes to hate a railway through its range: the more trains, the angrier it gets, until it waits beside your track, robs passing trains and freezes riders off their seats. An Umvuthana grove in the savanna becomes a tribe that sends an envoy for gold, then raptor hunters and a war band. Both come back after you kill them, both are left as Mowzie's made them if rtsim is removed, and a faction with nothing happening costs nothing.

A Frostmaw waits beside your track, robs the trains and freezes riders off their seats

This is the plan's own walkthrough. Iva lays 1,800 blocks of Create track from her copper mine, Copper Halt, to her smelter town, Smelter. It bends around a ridge and passes 150 blocks from a Frostmaw den she has never seen.

Distances from the plan. The map is a sketch; the close-up is to scale. Trains pass straight through a Frostmaw, so it attacks from beside the track.

Iva's line, day by day

  1. Day 0One train shuttles between the stations: 12 legs a day. Every pass through the range counts against Iva, the train's owner.
  2. Day 2She adds a second train: 24 legs a day.
  3. Day 2.7The grudge passes Notice. She is at the smelter, so the notice waits; the next morning, riding north into the range, she hears a roar.
  4. Day 7.5It passes 70: Raid. After a day's muster the Frostmaw leaves its den and waits 5 blocks off the rail, at the point of real track nearest its den.
  5. Day 8.8 to 10.8Trains that pass it while she is away lose cargo: at most 256 ingots over the two days, and never track. A roar at Smelter tells her a robbed train just came in.
  6. Day 9.5She rides north. An ice ball bursts beside her seat: she freezes for 2.5 seconds, is pulled off the carriage, and stands in the snow beside an awake Frostmaw while her train runs on. A swipe leaves a carriage's chest short.
  7. Day 11.1It goes home, bored. While the grudge stays at 70 or more it comes back 4 days after each return.

The Umvuthana: an envoy for gold, then hunters and a war band

Tomas builds a cattle ranch 150 blocks from a grove of 7 masked warriors, ruled by the Umvuthi, a sun-priest boss.

  1. The insultHe kills 4 warriors who chase him. That evening a gong sounds in the distance.
  2. 0.8 days laterAn envoy and two guards walk into his ranch, past his iron golem, and ask for 136 gold nuggets (about 15 ingots) within 3 days.
  3. PayHe right-clicks the envoy with the gold: 70% of the grudge goes and the tribe cannot climb for 6 days. His nuggets turn up in the grove's chests.
  4. RefuseRaptor-masked hunters come for his cattle and the villages' herds; at 70, a war band of 3 warriors and a raptor pack. Watched, it arrives in waves; while he is away, a fence all the way round stops it.
  5. PeaceHe wears an Umvuthana mask and the tribe leaves him alone, as Mowzie's does today. Trading with the grove while masked softens the grudge.
  6. End itHe kills the Umvuthi at its fire pit, shooting its cranes first: their Sunblock makes it untouchable. The bands run home, the tribe is cowed for about 6 days, and he takes the Sol Visage.

Raptor packs also plunder villages' leather and wool. Break one and villages within 2,000 blocks think better of you.

Trains a day set the grudge: 24 legs a day reach a raid in under a week, and cutting service calms it in days

Once a second, the Create plugin reads where every train is, loaded or not, and counts one pass each time a train crosses a range. No chunk is loaded and no track is read. Passes feed one meter per den and player, which forgets over about two days. One round trip a day is tolerated; each leg a day above that adds 8.66 to where the grudge settles.

Rungs at their grievance thresholds, to scale. A beast makes no demands, so the Frostmaw skips Envoy and Scouts. Neither faction breaks blocks, so neither has a Siege rung.

When a boss dies

  • The heir takes over at once, holding the grudges. The faction is cowed for 6 days, and the killer's feud steps down two rungs: for a Frostmaw, straight to Notice.
  • A stranger's kill clears nobody else's grudge. If Bram kills the Frostmaw at its den, Iva's feud stays; she gains only the empty days. Breaking a raid does end the raided player's feud.
  • The new Frostmaw holds the old grudge, so a line still busy is raided again soon after the cowed days.

Raids, envoys and tribute

  • A Frostmaw ambush, unwatched: each train passing within 8 blocks of its spot rolls a 1 in 2 chance. A hit destroys up to 64 items, at most 10% of the leg's cargo, at most once a train a day.
  • Watched: the real Frostmaw is awake at its spot once you are within 160 blocks, and its swipe takes cargo by the same roll.
  • A tribe raid, unwatched: fought out against your defences, and walls stop it. A raid that wins owes up to 2 dead animals per raptor pack, killed where they stand when your base next loads.
  • Envoys are safe: while a demand is open, golems leave the envoy party alone and every hit on it is cancelled unless a player struck. A player's blow is an insult.
  • Tribute is gold priced by value: 135 nuggets at the Envoy threshold; Tomas is asked 136 because his grudge stood at 30.2. Paying the Umvuthi its 7 gold blocks counts too.

The rates are placeholders until the population equilibrium design sets them. That design is now on main (EQUILIBRIUM.md, 3590249) and waits for your review on its own page. Under it a den farmed without pause yields about one Frostmaw a game year, not the 12 to 16 this plan's draft rates give, and a den left alone for three game years waits about as long as the first time. Whether a new Frostmaw takes 24 days or this plan's 6 is its question EQ-4. This plan adopts its rules once you approve them.

Remove rtsim and the dens and groves look as Mowzie's left them (UR-1)

UR-1 says rtsim can be removed at any time. Puppets, the bodies rtsim owns, are never saved by vanilla, so the plan keeps bodies vanilla wherever it can:

  • At home, bodies stay Mowzie's own entities, saved and untagged, with their health, crystal and masks.
  • A body becomes a puppet only while it is away. A war party's bodies leave a loaded camp when it sets out, and come back as saved vanilla bodies when it gets home: at once if the camp is loaded, else at its next load.
  • The census waits until every chunk of the camp has its entities loaded. Counting sooner would see an empty den, book its Frostmaw dead, and 6 days later spawn a second one with a second crystal.
  • Only a dead member's body is removed. A den that loads while its Frostmaw is out keeps the body and brings the Frostmaw home, unless a player is nearer to it than to the den.
  • A reset rebuilds each faction from its census: a robbed den stays robbed.

Removed on day 10, Iva at her smelter

The Frostmaw is waiting by her line, but its den has not loaded since it left, so the saved chunk still holds it. After the uninstall it is home asleep.

Removed on day 10, Iva watching it

Its den loaded while she watched it by the line, so its body became the puppet, and the den is left empty. This is the loss UR-1 accepts: members away whose bodies left a loaded camp, and the grudges.

Reinstalled on day 30, both factions rebuild from their censuses (crystal held, 7 warriors) with no grudges. No module's uninstall has been tested with puppets out yet; GameTest 4 covers this plan's.

Seven decisions for you, recommended option first

MZ-1 comes first because it blocks every conflict plugin, goblins included.

MZ-1: where the conflict module's Java half lives

Recommended: (a) a plain ConflictBridge in the core owns the conflict records and the territory map. The Mowzie plugin registers bodies, finds camps and counts them; the Create plugin samples trains.

Why: the first plugin to register a kind owns it, so if the Mowzie plugin owned the range, a later goblin plugin would collide with it.

Alternative

  • (b) The Mowzie plugin owns the conflict range itself.

MZ-4: after a boss dies, and which Mowzie's items stay (with MZ-7 and MZ-10)

Recommended: (a) heirs come back, the new Frostmaw after 6 days with its crystal. Keep Mowzie's drops, masks, trades and the crystal steal.

Why: dens stay dangerous and worth a visit, and the ice crystal and Sol Visage become renewable at one per heir. The pack's gun recipes need a supply of ice crystal (tacz_new_recipes.js:994), and stealing then killing still yields only one crystal per heir. This goes against the pack README's TODO to remove boss mods' items.

Alternatives

  • (b) The heir arrives without its crystal: no crystal renewal, and after its first death a den is melee only, so walls hold it and riders are safe.
  • (c) Heirs with crystals, but Mowzie's items removed as the README says: masks, trades and drops go, tribute is the only peace with the tribe, and stealing the crystal disarms but gives nothing.

MZ-5: what an ambush does to a train

Recommended: (a) cargo destroyed, at most 64 items a train a day and 10% of a leg, the same watched or not. Watched, it swipes passing carriages and its ice balls unseat riders.

Why: trains pass through a Frostmaw, so a bounded cargo loss is the cost that bites whether or not you are there, with no change to Create's trains.

Alternatives

  • (b) A forced stop: a mixin on Create's train holds it at the spot, so riders must face it and unwatched legs arrive late.
  • (c) A delay only: legs arrive late and no cargo is lost.
  • (d) Riders only, as in Mowzie's today: unwatched trains lose nothing.

MZ-13: whom a line's grudge falls on

Recommended: (a) the train's owner, the player who assembled it (Create's Train.owner); for a train with no owner, whoever spends most time at the line's stations.

Why: in multiplayer, the station rule alone can blame a bystander.

Alternative

  • (b) Always whoever spends most time in the stations' chunks.

MZ-11: the grove's people

Recommended: (a) the real warriors: saved as vanilla at home, counted each time the grove loads, tagged only while away.

Why: killing them at the grove shrinks its raids, and an uninstall leaves the grove as it was.

Alternative

  • (b) Leave them vanilla and keep a separate abstract pool: the grove looks the same whatever happens.

MZ-3: which bosses leave home

Recommended: (a) the Frostmaw only; you fight the Umvuthi at its grove.

Why: the Umvuthi stays a plain Mowzie's entity with its own memory of who traded with it. Leading raids would need a puppet form and moving that memory into the engine.

Alternatives

  • (b) The Umvuthi also leads the Invasion in person.
  • (c) Neither leaves home.

MZ-6: the tribe's tribute

Recommended: (a) gold nuggets, a new good, priced by value: half an emerald's worth per point of grievance, at 9 nuggets an emerald until markets set a price. That is 135 nuggets at the Envoy threshold.

Why: gold nuggets are already the grove's own trading currency on screen.

Alternatives

  • (b) Emeralds, as goblins ask: no new good.
  • (c) Gold ingots, priced the same way: 15 at the Envoy threshold, in coarser steps.
