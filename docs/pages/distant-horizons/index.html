Yes: everything you listed can show in Distant Horizons, as moving coloured boxes that cannot rotate

Research of 21 October for your ask: dragons out past render distance, trains and airships moving between bases, windmills still spinning, and smoke when war comes. It was read from the jars and the code. Nothing was built, run or measured, so frame cost, bandwidth and how smoke looks are estimates. You answered its four questions on 21 October (DH-1 to DH-4).

Distant Horizons 3.3.1, the version in the pack, lets a mod draw coloured boxes at any distance and move them every frame. The one hard limit is that boxes cannot rotate, so anything that turns (sails, wings, a ship changing course) is a flipbook of a few poses that swap. The first slice is trains: client only, through Create's own map sync, with no server or sim change.

Three limits to know up front

  • Players on the current pack may see no far terrain at all. DH 3.3.1 switches it off on any client with Chunky, and the pack ships Chunky on clients. DH 3.3.3 fixes it, and the pack is moving to it now (DH-3). More below.
  • Mac players see none of it. DH skips all box drawing on macOS.
  • Players who turn off DH's "generic rendering" (the switch for its beacons and clouds) see none of it either. The mod notices and asks the server for nothing.

Smoke, fire and scars under attack

Doable with sim work

What you would seeGlowing specks over the raided base or village, flickering for a while and going out, under a smoke column that lasts longer. Every player sees the same fire at the same time. When it is over, the scar stays: patches of burnt blocks over the raided ground.

HowThe engine knows every raid's place and time; it needs one new message per war event. When the raided chunk next loads, the sim applies the damage as now (broken machines, taken stores) and, by your answer, also swaps some surface blocks of the district or village for burnt ones, with no fire placed (DH-4). The scar is then real blocks, so it shows up close and in the far terrain. Whether DH passes a changed chunk on to other players' far views is not yet checked.

Up closeThe fire and smoke are drawn, never placed, so they end where your loaded chunks begin. The burnt blocks are real.

From above: rows of two along the road from the camp, a gold leader in the middle. The rows bob half a block out of step, so the column reads as walking.

First, far terrain: players on the current pack may see none, and the pack is moving to DH 3.3.3 to fix it

Distant Horizons 3.3.0 to 3.3.2 switch off far-terrain generation on any client that has Chunky installed. The config screen still shows it as on. The pack ships DH 3.3.1 and Chunky 1.4.23, both on clients and servers, so every player's client is affected: far terrain probably shows only where they have walked. This is confirmed in DH's source and the pack's config, but not checked in game.

Today: DH 3.3.1 with Chunky on the client

The client asks for no far terrain, in single player or from a server. Trains and dragons would float over a void, which is worse than no far view.

The fix: DH 3.3.3

The only newer DH for 1.21.1. It switches far terrain off only while Chunky is actually generating. It resets DH's config file, which the pack does not ship. Whether it breaks another mod in the pack is untested.

The other fix was to make Chunky server-only. You chose DH 3.3.3 (DH-3), Chunky stays on both sides, and an agent is updating the pack now. Nothing the far view needs is newer than DH 3.3.1, so the renderer works on either version, and no DH version, released or in development, adds rotation.

The plan: trains first, then one kind at a time

Each phase ends with something you can look at in game.

  1. 0

    Far terrain under way

    The pack moves to DH 3.3.3 (DH-3); then check that a client sees far terrain in multiplayer. Without it nothing below looks right.

  2. 1

    Trains, the first slice client only, no server or sim change

    • In rtsim's core, a client-side list of far objects that knows neither DH nor Create.
    • In the Create plugin, a client source that asks Create's map sync for train positions and copies them into that list.
    • The new renderer, mic_rtsim_dh: a group of boxes per carriage cut as a straight block or a staircase, one controller that moves them every frame, the test that hides them near the player, a detail setting, and the Mac and generic-rendering checks.

    Then measure the frame cost and bandwidth with 20 and 50 trains.

  3. 2

    Our own feed, and dragons

    Two network messages and a subscriber filter in core; a once-a-second train source replacing Create's all-trains stream; the engine's dragon leg message; real dragons near other players from the Ice and Fire plugin; the dragon boxes with wing poses; the hand-over to the real dragon. You approved the streaming this needs (DH-1).

  4. 3

    Windmills

    The capture in the Create plugin (sail shape, axis, speed) with its throwaway cache, and the pose cutter and flipbook on the client. Then the sim's word on whether the windmill's machines still stand, so the sails stop when they were destroyed (DH-2).

  5. 4

    War

    The engine's war event message from raids and ambushes; fire and smoke on the client, set on the ground height DH already knows; and the burnt-block swap when a raided chunk loads (DH-4). Then war bands on the march: the engine's route message, the column and leader boxes, hiding them when the real band appears, and the leader's handover from the Mowzie rework.

  6. 5

  7. 6

    NPC fleets, after they get bodies (P8), and far herds.

What it costs, estimated

Nothing here was measured. The engine gains no per-tick work: dragon legs, war events and marches are messages sent when they happen. Nobody has done this before

No mod draws Create trains, windmills or dragons through Distant Horizons. The only confirmed user of DH's box API is LOD Entity Rendering, for 1.20.1, which draws every entity as one box and sends every position to every player every tick; the far view filters and throttles instead. Separate Sable Render Distance stretches Sable's own range, which sends full ship data to far players; a silhouette is far cheaper. Nothing to reuse beyond DH's API itself.

You answered all 4 on 21 October

Logged as DH-1 to DH-4. Three as recommended; for raid scars you chose burnt blocks. DH-5 records that MZ-15 settles how the Umvuthi travels with its band.

1. May the server stream sim state to clients that opt in?

Your answer, as recommended: yes, opt-in clients.

Only to clients running the new renderer with DH's generic rendering on, filtered to their DH distance, at a detail setting they choose. This reverses PLAN §4.10's "No sim state is streamed" and adds mic_rtsim's first network messages and client code (DH-1).

2. How does I18 read for a far view?

Your answer, as recommended: show what eyes see.

For the far view, I18 now reads: it shows what your eyes would see from where you stand (movement, fire, smoke, sails turning or stopped), never numbers, names or anything you could not see from there. Windmills may stop when the sim says their machines were destroyed (DH-2).

3. How do clients get far terrain back?

Your answer, as recommended: update to DH 3.3.3.

It fixes the Chunky trap at its source, and Chunky stays on both sides. An agent is updating the pack now, so phase 0 is under way (DH-3).

4. Do raids leave scars?

Your answer: burnt blocks.

Not the recommendation, which was smoke and fire only. When a raided district or village next loads, the sim swaps some of its surface blocks for burnt ones, with no fire placed, so the scar stays visible near and far (DH-4).

Decided in the research; each can be undone

  • No test mod was built: the jars answered how to draw, whether rotation exists and what costs a frame. Frame numbers come in phase 1.
  • The feed and the client list live in rtsim's core with no pack mod in them; each source sits in its own mod's plugin; the renderer is its own client-only plugin.
  • The first slice reuses Create's map sync; phase 2 replaces it with our own filtered feed.
  • Rotation by flipbooks and re-cuts, not by replacing DH's shader, which would also take over its beacons and clouds and fight shader packs for the same slot.
  • Near the player, a proxy hides as soon as the real thing exists on the client, or its chunk is loaded.
  • NPC ships are not drawn until they have bodies. Smoke is solid first, see-through only once it is seen to work.
  • Bands mustering at camp or walking home are not drawn, since a player who rode up to one would find nothing there today.
