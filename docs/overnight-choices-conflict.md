# Overnight choices: the conflict engine (2026-10-20)

**Overnight agent 21.** Reversible choices made while Ben was asleep, one line each with the
reason, numbered K2-#. None is in DECISIONS.md, PLAN.md or OPEN-QUESTIONS.md; each can be undone or
put to Ben. Branch `overnight/conflict`. The work: PLAN.md §10.3 (C16 to C18, C24, C25) as a new
headless crate, `crates/rtsim-conflict`, with boundary records for a future plugin
(docs/boundary.md §9), `rtsim-cli sim conflict` and a faction sweep in `bench scale`. Every number
marked PLACEHOLDER is not measured. No Java.

## Shape

| # | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| K2-1 | **A new crate composed through public APIs**, as the dragons and the ships are: the caller owns the phase 1 `World` and an `Infrastructure` (rtsim-ecology's industry link) and passes them in; `Conflict::run_until` runs the world to each conflict event first. Havens are the world's haven nodes, marches are legs in its leg index, chiefs and heirs are its knowledge's notables, village raids are transfers between its markets, district raids go through the industry threat interface. | The brief; the dragons and ships showed the pattern works with save and restore. rtsim-ecology is a dependency only for its `Infrastructure` trait and `IndustryLink`. |
| K2-2 | **Cultures are JSON data** (`crates/rtsim-conflict/data/cultures/goblin.json`, `bandit.json`, `raider.json`, built in with `include_str!`; `Cultures::from_json` takes others): per signal a weight and a tolerated rate, forgiveness half-life, six thresholds, dwell per rung, demand terms, units (abstract body, threat class, strength, carry), each party's make-up, the warrior pool, speeds, patience, whom it hates, plunder. Every number is a PLACEHOLDER; the camp structure ids (`goblinstyranny:goblin_camp`, `illagerinvasion:illager_fort`) are UNVERIFIED; bodies are `mic_rtsim:conflict/<culture>/<unit>`. A fingerprint of the data is saved and checked on load. | "Culture tolerances from data"; P8 and P15 are open, so nothing names a mod's entity. |
| K2-3 | **Six signals**: smog, radiation, logging, mining, killing, building (the brief's list); their numbers are the boundary's. Destroy's pollution types map to them by `ConflictTunables::pollution` (smog and radioactivity at 1, greenhouse, ozone depletion and acid rain as half smog; type ids UNVERIFIED). | One vector per feud keeps the dominant cause known, which the demand names. |
| K2-4 | **P14's recommendation**: a feud per (faction, actor). A player's direct acts (a block broken or placed in a territory, ore, a member killed, an envoy attacked) count against that player; unowned signals (pollution, a district's emissions, an act with no player) count against one shared **settlers** actor, a knowledge `Group` actor with external id fnv1a64("mic_rtsim.conflict.settlers"). At the boundary, UUID (0, 0) means the settlers. | P14 is open; the brief says take its recommendation. |
| K2-5 | **Grievance is a closed form**: per kind, a level at an anchor and a constant inflow; `G(t) = G0·e + (r/λ)(1 − e)`, `λ = ln 2 / half-life`. The anchor moves only at events (a signal, a rate change, a rung, a tribute, a gift, vengeance); reads project. The next rung is booked at the closed-form crossing time (one tick of slack for rounding). f64, so composition holds up to rounding (property test) and the trajectory depends on events alone (test: reads change no state hash). | Flat catch-up in elapsed time (tested: the same events after 10, 1,000 and 100,000 days), O4's rule for floats. |
| K2-6 | **Tolerance applies to continuous signals only** (a rate below it causes nothing); impulses always count at their weight, and forgiveness handles the occasional act. | Tolerating "some logging" per impulse would need a per-actor running window: per-tick state or a second closed form for little gain. A culture that tolerates logging gives it a low weight. |
| K2-7 | **Emissions reach a faction** fully inside its territory and falling linearly to nothing over `reach_blocks` beyond it; impulses count only inside. | Smog from a refinery at the forest's edge is the walkthrough's case; a hard edge would make one block decide a war. |
| K2-8 | **The ladder**: one rung per event; up when grievance is at the next threshold, the current rung's dwell has passed, no party of the feud is out, no demand is open, the faction is not cowed and the pool can field the party; down one rung when grievance falls below 0.6 (hysteresis, data) of the current rung's threshold. **Temperament** (the chief's seed, C25) bends: thresholds down for an aggressive chief (up to 40 percent), tribute up (aggressive, 50), patience up (bold, 50), cowed time down (bold), gift relief up (sociable, 50). | Each step is a scheduled event; temperament makes two tribes of one culture differ with no stored state. |
| K2-9 | **District emissions are source rates** (`Conflict::set_emission`, keyed by district id and kind; 0 stops it). While loaded the Destroy plugin's POLLUTION records (amount over a window, per source) set them; while unloaded the composition should set them from the industry engine's learned rate of the pollution good (the industry-plugins branch writes pollution as deposit goods, its Q9): **not wired**, no composition runs conflict yet. In `sim conflict` the script sets the refinery's rate, and a Damaged district (a machine lost to a raid) stops it. | Rates keep tolerance meaningful and cost one event per change; the loaded and unloaded paths feed the same source. |
| K2-10 | **The demand**: tribute = ceil(grievance × `tribute_per_grievance` × the aggressive bend) of the culture's tribute good (emerald for all three; the haven's currency if the world lacks it), or stop the dominant signal. At the deadline: paid in full settles at once; **cut** if that signal's effective rate is 0 and no impulse of it came since the demand; otherwise **expired**: scouts if grievance is at the scouts' threshold, stay at envoy (no demand) above the hysteresis, else back to notice. An ENVOY_ANSWER refusal is an expiry now; an attack also counts as two members killed (PLACEHOLDER). | An abstract offer the plugin can show either way; both halves are checkable by the engine alone. |
| K2-11 | **A tribute paid in full buys a respite** (`respite_days`, data: goblins 6, bandits 5, raiders 4): the ladder holds at notice that long, and a tribute takes `tribute_relief` (goblins 0.7) of the grievance. With the signal still running the next demand is higher: a protection racket. | Without it a paid tribe came back half a day later; `sim conflict`'s tribute run pays four times in 24 days (17, 86, 96, 98 emeralds) and no band marches. |
| K2-12 | **Abstract district raid casualties**: of the attackers a raid did not leave standing (`1 − survived`, walls and defenders together), half fall (`abstract_loss_share`, PLACEHOLDER 0.5); the rest straggle home. | The industry outcome's surviving share folds the wall's gate in: attackers held at a wall are not all dead. |
| K2-13 | **District raids use the industry engine's per-class carry and block tunables**, whose defaults carry and break nothing for ground attackers (natural mobs, vanilla). The scenario's refinery sets ground melee 8 items and 1 block, ranged 4 items (PLACEHOLDER), as the dragons' farm sets flying carry. The culture's own `carry` is used for village raids only. **Proposal for Ben:** the threat interface could take carry and blocks per threat (from its source's units) instead of per class. | The threat interface is shared (C16 to C18) and its defaults are not this module's to change. |
| K2-14 | **Village and haven raids**: Lanchester's linear law of the band's strength against residents × 0.25 (villages) or 1.0 (havens) (PLACEHOLDERs), floored by the faction's own watched outcomes at comparable strength (within 2×): never better for the defender than observed (`village_outcome`, property-tested). Survivors carry at most their carry and at most `loot_share_milli` (goblins 150) of each good's stock, split by largest remainder (**revised by CONFLICT-LIFE.md CL-D58**: of each line's surplus over its target, and no fight when the band could not carry off one whole item). The goods move with a new `World::stock_to_held` (`crates/rtsim-world/src/raids.rs`: a market's stock into `Outside::held`, booked as a lump out) and land at the haven with `World::land_held` (no payment) when the band comes home. The village records a robbery report against the faction. | "Stock stolen to the faction's haven, conservation exact" (C24): both moves are internal to the conservation sum; checked after every event in a test. |
| K2-15 | **Loot from districts and trains** maps industry goods to the world's by name (the full id, then the part after `:`); unmapped goods are counted by name; it lands at the haven with `World::stock_from_players` (players' goods coming in), as the ships' district loot does. | The industry engine already booked them stolen; this keeps both books closed. |
| K2-16 | **Parties**: envoys and scouts always go and do not draw on the warrior pool; war parties need at least half their make-up from the pool or the feud waits for it (closed form). The war chief leads raids, invasions and sieges, one at a time. Survivors return to the pool. | A broken tribe cannot raid at once; the chief being out is what makes killing it matter. |
| K2-17 | **Routes**: A* over road edges (not lanes) from the camp to the node nearest the target, then straight to it; straight when no node is nearer than the camp. Nearest-node and plunder-target searches look at rings of 1,024-block cells outward. | "March on the road graph as legs"; the faction sweep found the first build's scans of every node. |
| K2-18 | **Leg owner bit 59** for parties, registered in `rtsim_world::interest::Viewer`'s docs (63 players, 62 dragons, 61 ships, 60 herds). `Conflict` implements `Viewer`; `observe_player` and `player_left` use the shared area (U2). Every party state with a place books a leg, waiting ones a stationary leg, so a player's area finds envoys and sieges at rest too. | One area per player, as the dragons and ships use. |
| K2-19 | **Watched** means a player reported within 2,400 ticks (PLACEHOLDER) is within the view radius of the target. A district the industry engine calls watched with no such player skips the raid (the industry's own rule). | The engine knows players' positions only through the shared areas. |
| K2-20 | **The raid director**: waves on an arc 112 blocks (PLACEHOLDER) around the watching player, at the bearing of the band's last waypoint, 30 degrees each side, at most 16 members in Minecraft (the spawn quota), a wave every 1,200 ticks while members wait, up to 6,000 ticks or boredom. Casualties bring the end forward in proportion; losing the culture's rout share routs it. The outcome joins the faction's observed fights. | The brief: boundary commands only; numbers wait for GameTests. |
| K2-21 | **A band seen on the march** is materialized at its place and steered waypoint by waypoint; it is recalled where its walk leaves the view (view radius + leg radius + 16, so a replanned area does not see it again at the border). Bands on their way home are never materialized. | The first build recalled by a fixed crossing time and flickered (materialized, recalled, materialized) while a band walked past a base. |
| K2-22 | **After the engine recalls a band** its counts are the truth: BAND_DEMATERIALIZED can only lower them (those that died on the way out), and casualties reported after it are ignored. A band embodied when it arrives somewhere unwatched is recalled before the abstract fight. | Found in the scenario: a plunder band seen on the way, then resolved abstractly, got its dead back from the plugin's survivor count. |
| K2-23 | **The war chief killed** (CHIEF_KILLED, op id): a killing report witnessed by the chief and the heir, then the chief dies in the knowledge (the heir inherits its strongest grudges, C24) and becomes chief; a new heir is named; the faction is cowed for `cowed_days` (goblins 6) bent by the new chief's boldness; the feud the band served (or the killer's) drops two rungs (PLACEHOLDER), never below notice; the band routs. Heirs' inherited grudges against players become killing grievance at half a point per sentiment point (PLACEHOLDER). | "Killing the war chief" de-escalates now and keeps the grudge for later. |
| K2-24 | **Plunder**: each culture that plunders draws exponential gaps (mean from data, bent by aggression) and sends plunderers at the nearest village of a culture it hates, or the nearest haven of a faction it hates (goblins hate humans and bandits; raiders hate everyone), within its reach. | "Relations between factions" with a consequence; rival havens are raided the same conserving way. |
| K2-25 | **Rescue news**: when a player routs a band, one rescue report (C24: rescues count; CL-D60) is witnessed first-hand only by the real witnesses (a village's is written into vanilla gossip by C20; a player base has no villagers and writes none): the village the band marched on, every village whose culture the faction hates within the view radius (160 blocks) of where the band broke (where the breaker stands for a watched fight, else where the band is on the road), and the player's base the band marched on and every player base within that radius (Ben: "Base carries the news"; the base tells the next travellers). A band routed on the road records the rescue too. CL-D61 replaced "every village within 2,000 blocks (PLACEHOLDER) whose culture the faction hates" with them; every other village hears it from travellers, at hearsay weight, and a rout nobody saw records no report. | PLAN.md's walkthrough: the village downriver that hates the goblins thinks better of the player. |
| K2-26 | **Kind ranges** 0x0090 to 0x009F, 0x0800 to 0x08FF, 0x8800 to 0x88FF (next to the herds' 0x0080, 0x0700, 0x8700); agent namespace 0x43 ("C"); dictionary kind 0x20 for pollution types; page table 0x4300 reserved for when the native engine routes conflict. | Checked against main and the herds branch: nothing else uses them. |
| K2-27 | **Every conflict command is per session** and none carries an op id: none moves items (tribute, gifts and loot are the plugin's records, with its op ids). | As the dragons' S11 and the ships' U4. |
| K2-28 | **Save**: everything, private fields included, grievance anchors as they are; the tunables and cultures are the caller's (fingerprint checked); the log is not saved; the faction and source indexes are rebuilt. Restoring in the middle of `sim conflict` (days 2, 9.3, 15.8, 21.5) gives the same state hashes and the same commands. | The persist pattern (S1, S8). |
| K2-29 | A feud at rung none with no inflow is forgotten below 0.5 (PLACEHOLDER), on its own booked event. | Feuds are rows; quiet ones should not stay forever. |
| K2-30 | `Conflict::order_party` sends a party now (tests, a plugin's GameTests, an admin order); `Conflict::traded` relieves a feud by a trade's value at a tenth of a gift's rate (PLACEHOLDER; not wired: phase 1's haven trades do not call it yet). | Test hooks the other modules have too. |

## Incomplete

- **No composition runs conflict**: rtsim-jni has no conflict module (no page table, no routing of
  0x08xx records, no Java dictionary for kind 0x20), and nothing feeds it from the industry engine's
  learned pollution rates (K2-9). Wiring it is the shape of `engine/ships.rs`. **Since wired**
  (`engine/conflict.rs`, the industry engine's learned pollution, K2-13's recommendation applied:
  choices C2-1 to C2-17 in docs/overnight-choices-conflictjni.md).
- **No Java**: no conflict plugin exists (P8). The fake plugin in `synth.rs` is the executable
  reference.
- **Supply-line ambushes** are implemented (a war band's segment crossing a booked train leg) but
  no scenario has trains, so they are untested.
- **Sieges** (repeated attacks until bored) are implemented but no run reaches the siege rung.
- **The watched fight's outcome** feeds only the faction's own table (village raids); the industry
  engine's floor for districts comes from the industry plugin's IND_FIGHT, which the scenario's fake
  world does not send for conflict bodies.
- **Faction relations** are only hatred from data (plunder targets, rescue news); no alliances, no
  wars between factions beyond plunder, no relations that change.
- **Phase 1 trades at a haven** do not call `Conflict::traded`.
